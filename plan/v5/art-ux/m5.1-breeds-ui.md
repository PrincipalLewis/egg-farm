## 1. Entry point: Flock button (Q-Art-1, BR-R1, plan R5)
**Where:** in the **Upgrades panel header**, right-aligned (between the `h2` "Upgrades" and Close on phone; flush right when docked, where there's no Close). It is **not a tab**: the segmented control keeps exactly 3 tabs (Collection · Farm · Workers), BR-AC3.
**Why there:**
- The header is already 56px tall and has a free gap of ≥ 82px between the title and Close at 360, so a 44px button fits **without changing any rect**. Measured: header, tabs, scene, #goal and Collect rects are identical with and without the button at all four sizes.
- Phone: the Upgrades sheet is an overlay (75dvh), and docked it lives in the right column, so the farm column (scene, #goal, Collect, streak row) can never move. That keeps layout-spec v1.5 intact.
- Plan R5's "tap the coop → Flock sheet" is overruled (Art owns entry points, R5): the coop + nest area is a Collect tap target (layout-spec §4.3), so a coop tap can't open a sheet without breaking Collect and the credited-tap rules (BR-R17b).
- "Coop panel" doesn't exist in the build. I read it as the Upgrades panel, whose Farm tab is the coop (BR-AC3's "there are 3 tabs" only makes sense there). Head confirmed this (Q-UI-1 resolved); PM rewords BR-R1 / BR-AC3.

## 3. Settling ring (Q-Art-4, BR-R4, 300 s)
- A 64px ring = a 4px band between 1px `--c-outline` edges. **Remaining** time is a `--c-focus` #2B79A8 arc (4.77:1 vs the white band and 4.44 vs cream). Elapsed time is white. The arc **drains** clockwise from 12 o'clock: `conic-gradient(var(--c-focus) 0 calc(var(--rem)*1turn), #fff 0)`, `--rem = remainingS / 300`.
- **Static:** JS sets `--rem` and the label once per second (on the sim second, ceil). There's no CSS transition, animation or pulse; `getAnimations()` on the slot is empty.
- Label `Settling m:ss` Nunito 12/700 `--c-muted` tabular-nums: `Settling 5:00` on placement, then `4:12` … `0:01`, never `0:00`. It runs on sim time (hidden < 60 s counts, offline doesn't) and freezes while offline.
- **Inactive trait:** sprite at 60% opacity, trait line `+15% away · inactive` 12px regular muted, and no family tag. Nothing is counted in Active totals.
- **Settled:** on the frame settling ends, the ring becomes a 3px solid `--c-meadow-strong` (4.9:1) ring, the trait goes to `--c-text` 700, and the family tag fades in (opacity 150ms; reduced motion: instant). No sound or pop. A polite live region says "Sussex settled" once.
- A breed moved between slots keeps its settled state (BR-R3): no ring restarts.

Esc and a scrim tap also close it.
- **Motion (≤ 400ms crack, then idle):**
  - 0–120ms: the egg (`icon-egg.svg` 72px) wobbles ±6°.
  - At 120ms: a 2.5px zig-zag crack line appears.
  - 160–400ms: the top half lifts up-left and fades. The hen (96px sprite) scales .8 → 1 with `--ease-pop`, starting behind the shell. The text block fades in over 240–400ms.
  - Duplicate: the new star fills at 400ms (scale 1 → 1.2 → 1, 200ms).
  - Card enter: opacity plus a .96 → 1 scale over 150ms. Nothing loops. No `#fx-layer` elements are used, so there's no FX-budget cost.
- **Reduced motion:** no crack, wobble or scale. The egg and hen **crossfade over 200ms (opacity only)**, the text fades with the hen, the duplicate star fills instantly, and the card enters with opacity only.

## 5. Highlight stagger (F10 unlocks Shift calls and breeds together)
1. **Shift calls go first.** The Flock entry's one-time highlight is eligible only when `abilitiesCoachSeen === true` (the "Shift calls unlocked!" coach card was dismissed with "Got it", worker-abilities spec §5) **or** the abilities flag is off.
2. It also needs F ≥ 10, `m4.flags.breeds` on, `flockHintSeen` false, and no other overlay open (onboarding, tracks explainer, a coach card, welcome-back, reveal, save dialog).
3. **Fires** the first time the entry is actually visible after eligibility (phone: the Upgrades sheet is open; docked: the panel is on screen): **2 glows over 1.2s** (`box-shadow 0 0 0 4px --c-yolk-dark`, in/out ×2, the §4.6c precedent). Then a static 12px `--c-barn` dot (5.26:1 on cream, 2px white keyline) sits top-right of the button **until the Flock sheet is first opened**, which saves `flockHintSeen = true`. It never repeats after a reload.
4. **Next-session fallback (Q-UI-2, approved):** if `abilitiesCoachSeen` is still false at the **start of the next session** after the session in which F10 was first reached (`abilities_unlocked`), the Flock highlight becomes eligible anyway. It fires as in step 3 the first time the entry is visible in that session. The calls card stays pending in the Workers tab and isn't dismissed.
5. One highlight per Upgrades open. If both the calls coach card and the Flock highlight are pending, calls wins, and Flock waits for a later open.
6. Reduced motion: no glow animation. A static 4px outline shows for 1.2s, then the dot.
7. Flock never sets the Upgrades nav dot or ★, and gets no Best-buy highlight (BR-BB1).

## 7b. Accessible names = visible text (v0.2.5, rev. 3.5 BR-AC52c, WCAG 2.5.3)
**Studio rule (HoAUX 23:40, going to PMC for BR-AC52c): the accessible name must START WITH the visible text; strict equality isn't required.** No `aria-label` may replace or reorder the visible text. Each control's name begins with its visible text in DOM order, and it may add hidden words after it (e.g. the chip's `.sr` state word). Decorative glyphs and icons (`+`, `🔒︎`, ring, sprites, stars) are `aria-hidden` / `alt=""` and are not part of the name. **State goes in `aria-describedby`** (a visually hidden node per control). Before v0.2.5 the slot/album controls had no written a11y spec; this section adds it. The wireframe source (`scratch/m51-breeds/www/wf.html`) was fixed to match; the images are visually unchanged.

## Obligations (OFL 1.1)
- Ship the copyright lines + OFL.txt with the fonts (e.g. `public/fonts/OFL.txt`).
- Self-hosting Google's woff2 files unmodified is fine. Our own subsetting counts as a Modified Version; that is allowed, and since neither font declares a Reserved Font Name, the names can stay.
- Fonts can't be sold on their own; bundling them in the game is fine.

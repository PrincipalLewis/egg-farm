**Checked, consistent (no conflict)**
- **Dashed state language:** the ZG chip is 1px dashed `--c-muted` and the Present paused chip is 1px dashed `--c-muted`. They are the **same on purpose**: the ZG doc §1 cites "the house pending/paused language". Recommend style-direction records "1px dashed `--c-muted` = not contributing now" (part of CH-1).
- **Line-through:** Present paused strikes `+X%`, while ZG has no strike. This is deliberate: there's a value to strike on the Present chip, but the ZG chip has no number.
- **Dashed colour token:** the album and slot dashed borders use `--c-slot-outline` and the chips use `--c-muted`. Both are #6B5D52; the alias split by role (cell outline vs text-ish chip) is fine.
- **Chip contrast:** ZG 5.91 (cream) and Present 6.34 (white) both use `--c-muted`; the figures differ only because of the background.
- **Icon sizes and CSS:** 14px card (vertical-align −2px) and 11px HUD (−1.5px). They match their signed sections.
- **Opacity elsewhere:** breeds silhouette 0.55 (3.33:1), ZG no opacity (UI-ZG-3), m9 `.ebar` .5. Consistent with "no text below opacity 1".

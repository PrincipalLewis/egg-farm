**Copy** (ruling 4): `This won't raise income now.` · `Buy anyway` · `Cancel`.

**Behaviour** (v1.1: Buy is a disclosure toggle, ruling A3)
- **Closed** (default): the strip is `hidden` and Buy has `aria-expanded="false"` and `aria-controls="zgc-<key>"`.
- **Open:** the first Buy activation (tap, Enter or Space) opens the strip, sets `aria-expanded="true"` and does not buy. **Focus** moves to `Buy anyway`.
- **Re-tap closes:** a Buy activation while the strip is open **closes it** (A3). It sets `aria-expanded="false"`, focus **stays on Buy**, and nothing is bought.
- **Other ways to close:** Cancel, Esc (anywhere in the strip) or a pointerdown outside the strip and Buy. Each one closes the strip and focuses the row Buy with `aria-expanded="false"`.
- One strip at a time: opening another closes the first, and the first Buy goes back to `false`.
- **Double-tap guard (new in v1.1, see Q-ZG-B1):**
  - Before the guard, in **docked** layout (Cr/FF, 960×600 and 1280×800), `focus()` on `Buy anyway` scrolled the panel body by 193px and 93px. Buy moved out from under the finger, so the second tap of a fast double-tap landed on the strip, which could include `Buy anyway`. WebKit and phone sizes didn't scroll.
  - The fix is 3 parts:
    1. `focus({preventScroll:true})`.
    2. Reveal with `scrollIntoView({block:'nearest'})` **350ms** after opening.
    3. `Buy anyway` and `Cancel` ignore activation for **350ms** after opening.
  - Re-measured: the scroll after opening is **0** at every size and engine. A second tap 60ms after the first hits Buy's own `span.num`. Log: `[open, close:buy-retap]`: it closes and never buys.
- **Keyboard guard proof (v1.2, B1):** `scratch/m51-zg/zgk.mjs` → `zgk-{chromium,firefox,webkit}.json`, run at 360×640, 960×600 and 1280×800 in each engine. The wireframe JS needed no change: its guard is on the `click` activation, which Enter and Space also dispatch.

| Input on the strip after Enter-opening | at +80ms | at +450ms |
|---|---|---|
| Enter on Buy anyway | `guarded:buy-anyway`, stays open, focus Buy anyway | `buy-anyway`, closes, focus row Buy |
| Space on Buy anyway | guarded, same | buy-anyway, same |
| Enter on Cancel | `guarded:cancel`, stays open | `close:cancel`, focus row Buy |
| Space on Cancel | guarded, same | close:cancel, same |
| Space on Buy, then Space again at +60ms | `open, guarded:buy-anyway` | — |
| Enter held on Buy (synthetic repeat keydown at +100 / +600ms) | `open` only, no activation | — |

  - *(v1.2 caveat, now resolved by C1 — see the next bullet.)*
- **Key-repeat guard proof (v1.3, C1):**
  - **Wireframe JS v1.3:** `keydown` on `Buy anyway` and on `Cancel`: `if(e.repeat&&(e.key==='Enter'||e.key===' ')){e.preventDefault();}`. It doesn't stop propagation, so the app's own handlers still see the event. Tab, Esc and all other keys are untouched.
  - **Finding:** the build already has an app-wide rule, **PL-1** (`src/ui/app.ts:291–294`). It calls `preventDefault()` on any repeated Enter/Space keydown whose target is inside a `button`. So on 30b29d3 a held key could not confirm even before v1.3. The strip guard is a local, explicit copy of PL-1, so C1 holds even if PL-1 changes.
  - **Tests** (under flock, no server left running). Chromium uses real CDP `autoRepeat` keydowns; FF and WK use Playwright repeat keydowns.
    - **Held Enter for 2s** (`zgh.mjs` → `zgh-*.json`): press Enter on the row Buy and hold it for 2s, with a repeat every 33ms (55–58 repeats). Result: the strip opens once, then `repeat-ignored:buy-anyway` × every repeat. Nothing is bought (`collectionLevel` 20, coins unchanged), the strip stays open with `aria-expanded="true"`, and focus stays on Buy anyway. 0 of the repeats reached the document un-prevented. That holds in Cr, FF and WK at 360×640 and 1280×800.
    - **Pure repeat keydown, Chromium CDP** (`zgr.mjs` → `zgr-chromium.json`): a lone repeat of Enter or Space on Buy anyway or on Cancel, 600ms after opening (past the 350ms guard). Every case is ignored (`repeat-ignored:*`), with no click and no close.
    - **Control:** a fresh, non-repeat Enter after 350ms still confirms (`buy-anyway`) in every engine.
    - **Pre-v1.3 JS:** the same held-Enter run with the pre-v1.3 JS also didn't buy. PL-1 prevented every repeat (`zgh-*-pre.json`).
- **Tab order:** row Buy → `Upgrade Farm →` (the Limited-by-coop link) → strip. Shift+Tab from `Buy anyway` lands on the link, not on Buy (measured in all 3 engines). That's acceptable: the strip is the row's next sibling, and Esc returns to Buy.
- `Buy anyway` buys only if coins ≥ cost at that moment (E16). Otherwise it does the normal refused buy and mirrors `aria-disabled`.
- Motion: 150ms fade and expand; instant under `.rm` or reduced motion.

## 9. Spec and brief conflicts (rulings win)
- **C1 (spec §1, §6, ZG-AC4):** the spec asks for a `+0` / `+0 income` label and a "visibly de-emphasised" row. → Ruling 1–2: a `No gain now` chip, no `+0` anywhere, and only the effect line and chip muted. PMC should update §1, §6 and ZG-AC4 ("the Art-final ZG label" = `No gain now`, "muted class" = `.zg`).
- **C2 (spec §6 "accessible name includes the no-gain fact", ZG-AC4 "accessible name contains the no-gain phrase"):** → Ruling 8: no aria-label. The phrase is in the row text (chip) and in the Buy **description** (`aria-describedby`). The Buy's accessible *name* stays the cost. ZG-AC4 should say "row text contains `No gain now`, and the Buy's accessible description is `No income gain now. Buying won't raise income.`"

## Findings
- **m1 Minor — the yard rebuilds every sprite on a Hens buy.** `src/ui/app.ts:534` `yard.innerHTML = slots.join('')`. After a Hens buy, 0 of 5 (or 0 of 10) old `img.hen` nodes survive (keptIdentity 0 at every width). I saw no flash: imgs are decoded from cache, the count never drops, and old sprites have no `.pop`. But every old hen restarts its `bob` animation (`styles.css:84`), so the 2px idle bob re-syncs once per buy. The line is untouched by #69 and was the legacy behaviour on Farm buys. #69 moves the trigger to Hens buys. Fix (follow-up, non-blocking): build the 12 `<span>` slots once, then on a count change append an `<img class="hen pop">` only into the newly filled slots (and remove from emptied slots on a decrease).

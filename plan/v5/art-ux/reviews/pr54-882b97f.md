### Unlock-level derivation (at 882b97f)
- `data/economy.csv`: `egg_value_cap_over_farm` = 3 on all 31 rows (L0–L30); `hens_cap_over_farm` = 3.
- `src/config/balance.json`: `maxLevel` 30, `m4.enabled` 1, `m4.eggValue.capOverFarm` 3, `m4.hens.capOverFarm` 3. These are synced from the CSV (`scripts/sync-economy.ts:170`).
- `src/config/formulas.ts:116`: `eggValueCap(F) = min(maxLevel, F + capOverFarm)`.
- `src/sim/tracks.ts:21–25`: `trackLock` returns `'cap'` when `V >= trackCap`.
- `src/ui/tracks-ui.ts` `trackUnlockFarm` returns the first F' > F with `trackCap(F') > V`.
- Unlock table for V9: F5 → cap 8 (locked) · F6 → cap 9 (locked, since 9 ≥ 9) · **F7 → cap 10 (unlocks)**. So n = 7, which equals max(F+1, V − 3 + 1). The browser agrees: locked at F5/F6, Buy button at F7. The old F+1 rule would have said L6 at F5, and buying F6 would not have unlocked the row.

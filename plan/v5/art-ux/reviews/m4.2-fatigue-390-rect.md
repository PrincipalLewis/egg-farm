## Fit (text natural width / space to the slot's right edge, wrap probe with wrapping allowed)
- `Tired −$999.9K/s` (text 104.3 px, pill 148.3): **fits on 1 line at 360 (pill right 206.3 ≤ 352) and 390 (206.3 ≤ 382)**, all 3 engines. No clipping, no overlap with `#rate`, coins or basket pills.
- `Tired workers −$999.9K/s` (text 157.8, pill 201.8): **fits on 1 line at 400 (259.8 ≤ 388) and 1280 (418.2, settings at 1224)**, all 3 engines. It would also fit at 360/390 (259.8), so the <400 short form is a choice, not a need.
- Contrast: text `--c-wood-text` #8A5528 on cream **5.74:1**; Asleep `--c-danger` #B83B37 on cream **5.26:1**; pill border on cream 12.31:1. The spec's "amber text" (#F29E1F on cream ≈2.0:1) fails AA, so the errata names `--c-wood-text` as the amber-family text token; the amber cue is carried by the yellow `icon-warning.svg`, and state is never colour alone (icon + words; Asleep keeps the same icon and words, only the colour changes).
- Hit area 148.3–201.8 × 44 everywhere (≥44×44). At ≤400 it spans y 58–102 / 60–104: clear of the HUD bottom (56) and ends at the queue top (102 / 108), no overlap.

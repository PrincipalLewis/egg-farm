**Required before implementation (blocking):**
1. **B1 Free swaps defeat the strategic slots.** BR-R4 lets a player put Present breeds in while playing and swap to Away a second before closing. The best play is then "always swap", so the families stop being a choice (CEO Q5) and become a chore. BR-AC22 only tests static loadouts. **Fix:** a breed placed in a slot "settles" for `bc_settle_s` of sim time before its trait applies, and the Away grant uses only breeds settled at the moment of leaving. Add an AC.
2. **B2 Incubator needs an absolute sim clock that RC1 doesn't save.** `startedSimMs`/`readyAtSimMs` assume a persistent sim-time counter; `GameState` has none (`state.ts`). E11 also contradicts this ("timer doesn't advance" with an absolute ready time). **Fix:** store `remainingMs`, decrement by sim dt, decrement by the away span in the offline grant, and complete at ≤ 0. Rewrite BR-R8/R18/R20, E11, E12, BR-AC8/AC25 and Q-HoE-1.
3. **B3 Column count and a non-numeric column.** V4, and `bc_rarity_w` "70/22/7/1" is a string in a numeric CSV. **Fix:** split it into four numeric columns and recount.
4. **B4 BB-3 wording:** "at L30 MAX there is never a ★" is false if a worker hire or a Grader tier is still offered with a gain. **Fix:** say "no ★ unless a worker hire or Grader tier still has a gain".

6. **B6 Pity and the free hatch:** whether the guaranteed-Common free hatch counts toward pity is undefined. **Fix:** state it (it counts as a non-Rare+ draw).
7. **B7 Model sha (V6) and the count (V4).**

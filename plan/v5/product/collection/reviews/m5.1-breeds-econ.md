## 2. Q-Eco-2: is a hatch an event at L30 MAX? **Yes. Count it.**
- **Why (SCRATCH, M5.0 draft, maxLevel 30, idle hatcher Farm-first, seeds 1–200, `out/hatcher/e3-l30max.log`):** without breeds the idle player at L30 MAX has nothing meaningful to buy: the longest gap until T runs 3,618 / 3,680 / 3,729 s (P50/P95/max; Collection excluded). With hatches counted, the longest meaningful gap from F28 until flock complete is **227 / 247 / 268 s, and it's the same pre-MAX Farm wait as no-breeds**. Gaps after MAX are shorter than that (a hatch every 60 s incubation + ≤ 45 s of income). The flock completes at a median **77.7 min** (199/200 seeds), so about 28 min of L30 MAX play gets an event cadence.
- **Gate/pacing rationale:** at L30 MAX the hatch is the *only* spend that changes the game state (it adds lay/offline/Broody value and moves the album). The hatch costs 45 s of planner income = $125,087 (MODEL `closed_form.py`) = 38.6% of the last paid step (`farm_cost[29]` $323,719) and 20% of row 30 ($624,316). That's above the E3 "≥ 10% of a step" bar, so it's a real decision, not a trivial tap. Counting it is honest because each hatch has a cost, a wait and a payoff.
- **Limits:** after flock complete, L30 MAX is a dead zone again (back to a ≈3,600 s gap). Breeds don't fix F7. Note too that the 247 s pre-MAX P95 gap is an M5.0 idle-bot baseline (Farm waits, Collection excluded), not a breeds effect; it belongs to PMFU's BB-9/L30+ work.
- **Don't count it in the ★ / BB-9 meaningful rule** (BR-BB, PMFU co-sign): count it in G4 "something to do" only at MAX. Below MAX a hatch delays the Farm step, so it isn't progress.

### V-2d `dead_zone` definition (B-1 CLOSED rev. 11; item (1) revised rev. 14)
**Source of record: Econ 14:18 (Econ → PMC, PMFU, HoQA), items (1)–(6); agreed by PMFU (D-PMFU-2026-10-30-BB10, 14:22); B-1 CLOSED by HoQA, QA-D-1030-TELERR6.** **Item (1) revised rev. 14 per D-HOP-2026-11-05-12** (co-signs QA-D-1105-MRINTERP2 `qa/m5/interp-mr-accounting-v2.md` sha256 `2a5211029a63d64b5ace5391544702b55640a92fd82eea1f82e2f499be631a68` §2.2; Economy countersign D-ECON-20261105-MRINTERP2 sha256 `36c01a4b33121a6f7e415d7b57c4a85defe87b40942a8a27c8bf276265e83df1`). 1. **(rev. 14 — revised; D-HOP-2026-11-05-12 / QA-D-1105-MRINTERP2 §2.2; (a) only, (b) deferred.)** The emitter (and the harness of record) samples meaningful affordability as follows:
   - (i) on every check-in / sell tick: **after collect taps and `sellAll`, before any buy**, at that sim time (**fractional sim times allowed**);
   - (ii) on every integer sim second that is **not** a check-in tick: at that integer second with current coins (unchanged from rev. 11–13).
   - When a check-in falls on an integer second, **only** the post-`sellAll` sample is taken (no pre-sale sample at the same `t`; dedupe).
   - Affordable / meaningful / stretch start–end / `s` / `farm` attribution otherwise remain as in V-2d (2)–(3). **Purchases do not themselves end or reset a stretch.** The 30 s emit floor, P95 rule, and `none_affordable` reason are unchanged. The **180 s** gate bar is unchanged.
2. Affordable means some `candidates()` row has coins ≥ cost and is meaningful. Meaningful is key FARM or W1–W4, or `optionGain.gain` > 0 (`assumeWorking`; Q-E2 collect when bestBuyV2 = 1). Zero-gain rows and hatches below MAX never count.
3. A stretch starts at the first non-affordable **sample** (`farm` = the level at that sample) and ends at the next affordable **sample** or at max. `s` = end − start (**fractional starts/ends allowed** when the opening/closing sample is a fractional check-in). The L29→30 buy is inside gap(29). A harness stretch still open at T is closed at T; the telemetry equivalent is the unload flush with `open:true`. **Purchases do not themselves end or reset a stretch ((b) deferred).**
4. 30 s is a telemetry emit filter only; the harness has no floor. An open stretch under 30 s at unload is **not** emitted (settled by Econ).
5. D-HOP-49's P95 is a harness metric: per seed, the longest stretch from F10 to max, seeds 1–200 per profile, using the `pacing-guards` quantile. It is not the raw distribution of events.
6. The harness measures only `none_affordable`.

**Telemetry-only points (PMFU, D-PMFU-2026-10-30-BB10):**
- (a) On unload or background, emit once with `open:true` and `s` so far, only if `s` ≥ 30. Sampling starts fresh on resume.
- (b) Emitter scope 10 ≤ farm < 30, **sampled exactly as Econ (1)** (an emitter that samples differently breaks the one-to-one check). `reason` is pinned to the single value `none_affordable`; `cap` and `max` are dropped.
- (c) **No P95 comes from telemetry**: the ≥ 30 s filter censors the distribution. The only P95 is the blocking one (D-HOP-49 (H2)): over each seed's longest harness stretch, 200 seeds per profile, `pacing-guards` quantile.

## Major
- **MJ1. The first post-migration offline grant gets the Hens offline multiplier for free.**
  - M4A-30 (line 184) applies the offline grant after migration, using the migrated `hensLevel`. M4A-4 (line 69) multiplies offline eggs by the Hens milestone.
  - Because H = F at migration, every save at F ≥ 10 gets ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×2.0 offline eggs on its first welcome-back (r2 §3: "offline eggs/s jump … max ×2.0 at F30"), on top of the V0 price uplift.
  - The 1.5× income cap (line 174) measures coins in the first 60 s after load, and it doesn't say whether the offline grant counts. At F20 and above the grant alone is ×1.5–2.0.
  - **Asks:** state whether the grant is in the metric. Either compute the absence's grant at v6 stats, or accept it explicitly as part of the burst.
- **MJ2. Spec hole: does the offline cap scale with the offline multiplier?**
  - In the CSV, `offline_max_eggs_2h` equals `offline_eggs_per_sec` × 7200 on every row (L10: 2.75 → 19800; L30: 7.75 → 55800).
  - If the cap isn't multiplied, the ×2.0 offline milestone only means reaching the same cap in 1 h. That makes it worth nothing on a ≥2 h absence.
  - M4A-4 also says offline uses "the same H-based eggs/s" without naming the column (`eggs_per_sec` or `offline_eggs_per_sec`).
  - **Asks:** name the column, and say whether `offline_max_eggs_2h` × hens_ms_offline_mult applies.

## Minor
- **mn1. Hens is now the default best buy, but the caps keep it from dominating.** It leads ROI at 23 of 25 levels (§1). The caps (F+3) force switching, and buy shares are even (§2). That fits M4A-39, but most choices are now "Hens until capped, then Egg value, then Farm".
  - In this model Farm never has direct ROI at H = F, because supply sits below demand at every F (§1: dem > sup(H=F)). Farm's value is lifting the caps.
  - Not a defect. It would be worth checking with workers in `tr5_run.py`, since W3 changes demand.
- **mn2. The coop milestone gives almost nothing to an active player.** It only matters if the coop fills between visits. M1 deliberately moves Hens value to idle and coop, so this is an intent check, not a bug. Claude raised the same point.
- **mn3. Pre-patch Collection buying is a mild windfall.** Buying cheap Collection levels before the update (collection_cost < farm_cost for L0–L10, r1 mig_edges_out) yields V0 up to 12 at F0. The player keeps the Collection levels (eggs per tap) and also gets Egg value levels without paying `egg_value_cost`. It is price-neutral by design and low-level only. Accept, but note it.
- **mn4. The income-cap AC (line 174) still uses only the C = F−3 / C1 fixtures.** Add F12/C1, F16/C0 and F23/C29, the top three by ratio in §3.

## (b) 60-min dead zone at F23–F26 (known; not a new finding)
- My model shows the same waits: greedy has 197 / 188 / 236 s at F24–F25, and 246 s at demand ×0.75. Casual at demand ×0.75 has one wait of 196 s at F23 (§2). Three of the four longest greedy waits are Farm buys.
- **My view on the fix options:**
  1. **Prefer a cut to `farm_cost` at F22–F26** (about −25 to −30%), with a smaller or no cut to `egg_value_cost`. Farm is where the waits are, and Farm also lifts the caps.
  2. Economy's flat −25% on both tracks (response M3 row) risks speeding up Egg value, and so Balanced and price, more than needed. It also gives R2 (L22 at 30 min, top of the band) no margin if any F21 row moves.
  3. Re-run `tr5_run.py` with workers before choosing. Workers and the Grader likely shorten these waits, and my no-worker model overstates them.
  4. Raising demand won't work: in-tab supply, not demand, binds (§1).
  5. Gating only 30:00 doesn't validate this range at all.

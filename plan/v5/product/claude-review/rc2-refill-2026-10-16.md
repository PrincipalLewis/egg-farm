Claude Design Reviewer, advisory only. Nothing was edited. Pass `rc2-refill-2026-10-16`.
Input: `product/workers/rc2-refill-proposal.md` v0.2 (L = line). Context: `workers/m4-abilities-spec.md` L12–25, `workers/rc1-asleep-state-spec.md` L6, `m4-tracks-model.md` L181, and CSV constants from `product/economy.f1-m4cols.csv` row 0: drain_s_per_pct 9, tired_below_pct 30, yawn_at_pct 40, manage_cooldown_s 30, tc_ability_s 30 / cd 300, worker unlocks F3/7/8 and Grader from F18.
Script: `claude-review/scratch/rc2_refill.py` → `rc2_refill_out.txt` (§1–§4). Claude: `claude_rc2_prompt.txt` → `claude_rc2_call1.txt`.
The model is deterministic: 1% energy drain per 9 s, and a refill at the threshold restores the worker to 100%. It runs no Monte Carlo, so AC-RF5 and AC-RF8 still need Economy's sim.

## Blocker
None. The proposal is v0.2 and not yet simmed.

## Major
- **MJ1. Charges are never scarce, so Refill still isn't a decision (it fails the brief at L2).**
  - Supply is 1 charge/min (L11). Demand at the 40% threshold is 0.33/min with 3 workers (F8–F17) and 0.44/min with 4 (F18+). It reaches 0.67/min only with 4 workers at Market Hand Focus 1.5× drain (out §1). That confirms L22's 0.44.
  - Coming back to the tab doesn't create pressure either. Workers drop to ≤40% only after 540 s, and when they're in phase you're short by at most 1 charge. That worker waits about 60 s for the next charge, while it would take 360 s to fall asleep (out §1–§2).
  - **Refill all (L59) puts back the chore it was meant to remove.** It refills in risk order, which is the same ranking the decision rows use (L13). It also keeps workers in phase. The optimal play becomes "tap Refill all when lit", which is the rc1 problem (L5).
  - Asks:
    - Have Economy sweep charge_regen_s at 90–120 s (out §1 shows 120 s undersupplies only the Focus case) or charge_cap at 2.
    - Or make a charge restore less than 100%, so choices compound.
    - Show that some player model ends up genuinely charge-constrained.
- **MJ2. The combo rule pushes the active/idle stack toward the CEO's 2× cap.**
  - Today's bound is 1.84× (stack 1.62 × all-ability 1.136; `m4-tracks-model.md` L181). Linear scaling of ability uptime gives 1.95× at a 45 s Boost and **2.06× at the 60 s top of the range (L40)** (out §4).
  - How many combos a player can actually land is limited:
    - each worker can be refilled at most once per 540 s (60% × 9 s);
    - a worker can be held at ≤40% while still producing, for 90 s before it turns Tired;
    - so a timed player can combo about every other Boost.
  - That makes real uptime about 12.5%, not 15% (my estimate: 45 s + 30 s per 600 s). This gives about ×1.17, a ~1.89× bound.
  - Even so, **the top of the combo_boost_duration_s range shouldn't ship**. Cap the range at ≤45 s and require AC-RF5's worst-seed 2× check to include the combo.
- **MJ3. Key behaviours are undefined.**
  - Does energy drain while offline? Workers produce nothing offline, and charges don't regenerate offline (L11). If energy doesn't drain either, L22's "come back to several tired workers" only happens with the tab open and idle.
  - "Offline" isn't defined for a hidden or throttled tab, or for clock changes. Ability cooldowns tick offline (`m4-abilities-spec.md` L16, AC-A4), but charges don't, which is inconsistent.
  - It's unclear whether you can Refill a Rested (paused) worker, or what Rest does while a Boost is running (abilities can't run while paused, L12 of the abilities spec).
  - Tie order in the risk sort, and whether rows re-sort while the player is tapping.
  - What "$/s at risk" means (L16, AC-RF3): gross incomePerSec, net of wage (L24 says W1's wage ≈ its value), or the Grader's loss after it keeps 15% (`rc1-asleep-state-spec.md` L6).

## Minor
- **mn1. "Tired" means two things.** The Tired state is 1–29% (`m4-abilities-spec.md` L13), but Refill eligibility starts at ≤40% (L12), and "time until Tired" (L14) doesn't say which. The ≤40% window opens 90 s before Tired (out §1), so a patient player times refills at about 31% for no loss. That's harmless, but it makes 40% an unspent margin.
- **mn2. Rest is free and close to "fire them".** Recovery takes 36 / 42 / 60 min from 40 / 30 / 0% (out §3). A worker Rested at 40% is no longer refill-eligible after 0.6 min. For W1, whose wage ≈ value (L24), permanent Rest is the dominant line for active sellers, which repeats the pause-sweep result (`m4-tracks-model.md` L94).
- **mn3. Several ACs are untestable or vacuous.**
  - **AC-RF1** (L43) says there's one Refill entry point, but row Refill (L18) and Refill all (L59) are also Refill controls. Spell out what the DOM scan should match.
  - **AC-RF5's 25% proxy** (L51/L63) measures the sort order and the bot's policy, not a decision. The bot policy isn't specified.
  - **AC-RF6** (L52) has no time bound, so "never stuck" passes even though a 60 min Rest does the job.
  - **AC-RF8's dead zone** (L62) is tautologically absent ("tired + charges + can't act" can't happen), and "idle loss" has no defined player model.
- **mn4. Stale text.** L8 removes bulk refill, but v0.2 L59 puts it back. Telemetry (L26–31) has no `refill_all_used`.
- **mn5. Keeping a full cap is cheap.** Regeneration while capped is wasted (L31), but an open tab farms charges with no cost. That's fine as long as charges are worth little (see MJ1).

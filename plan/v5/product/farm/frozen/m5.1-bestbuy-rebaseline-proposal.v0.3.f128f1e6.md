## 2. What changes (M5.1)
1. **Part A, shared by the UI and the bot** (`econ/best-buy.ts` / `ui/pick-hysteresis.ts`, used by the UI picker and by `tests/bot-m4.ts` `pickM4All`):
   - Zero-gain rows (today: Collection) never get the hint, highlight or ★. Farm, workers and Grader are exempt (BBv2-1).
   - The 0.9× hold never protects a zero-gain pick (BBv2-2).
   - An all-∞ tie goes to Farm, not the cheapest (BBv2-3).
   - When nothing useful is affordable, the strip counts down to the best useful buy, with no ★ (BB-5).
2. **Economy's collect fix (Q-E2):** once a Coop Hand is hired, best-buy models collection at 0 player taps (collect = `eggs_per_tap[C]` / `w2_every_s`, `assumeWorking`), so Collection gets its real value for an idle player. Before the hire, 1 tap/s stays.
3. **Dropped:** Part B (payback + time to afford: about 40 s, 1–2%, more pick churn) and the BB-9 L29→L30 exception (the step is $323,719, or 1:56 from an empty bank; the earlier $624K was off by one). UC17 is retired, replaced by BB-13 (on a fresh save, the first ★ is a meaningful buy affordable within 30 s).
4. **M5.0 is unaffected:** the M5.0 ★-only stopgap (`bb-stopgap-spec.md` v1.2.1) doesn't touch the bot, and AC7 hashes stay identical.

## 3. Why
Vadim's playtest and the Critic's L10+ read (`product/critic/l10-interaction-read-2026-10-23.md`) found dead zones of 56 s at L17, about 2:15 at L28 and about 1:55 at L29, with ★ pointing at Collection, which delays Farm.
- **Cause:** the ★ picker lets a buy with zero income gain win.
  - Collection adds no income in the M4 model; all 5,000 bot buys on seeds 1–200 had gain 0.
  - Its bundle borrows Farm's payback, and affordable-first lets it win.
  - The 0.9× hold then keeps ★ on it. At L17 with $37.3K banked, ★ stays on Collection indefinitely.
  - At L28–29 every option ties at ∞, and the tie goes to Collection as the cheapest.
- **Measured (real sim, from the Critic's L10 save, following ★):**
  - Today: 13 zero-gain buys, L10→L30 in 39:27, with waits of L17 1:21, L28 2:36 and L29 3:48.
  - With Part A: 32:03 (−19%), and no wait over 2:09 up to L30.
  - Worst seed L28–30 under A: 2:54.
- **Why the bot must change too:** D-HOP-2026-10-19-02 defines both pacing bots as ★ followers. Fixing only the UI leaves the gates modelling a player who makes 20–25 Collection buys the UI now tells them not to. So the gates would overstate ★-follower time to L10 and L30 by about 17–20% (Economy Q-E5).

- **(a) New L10 window, centred on the Part A bot: 7.0–10.0 min** (fastest ≥ 7.0, P95 ≤ 10.0). SCRATCH: fastest 7.50/7.56, P50 8.4, P95 9.2–9.4. This is the CEO's own D-HOP-07 follow-up ("revisit the window"). Economy confirms the exact bounds on gate runs.
- **(b) L18 sanity band** (`l18_minutes_sanity`, non-gating): 19.8 → **≈ 17.3–18.3 min**.
- **(c)** The AC7 L30-at-30-min band 18–22 is kept.
- **(d)** `ac7_w_t20_p95_warn_s` re-centred (t20 P95 25:56 → about 22:59).
- **(e)** New AC7 hashes, published by Economy and adopted as the baseline for SS-27, F4-25 and BR-BB5.
- **(f) Freeze amendment, narrow:** L0–30 play may differ from main **only** through the M5.1 Best buy change (§2.1–2.2). economy.csv rows 0–30 stay frozen, and G7 is still checked cell-wise.
- **(g) BB-10 gate definition: G4 counts meaningful rows only (D-HOP-2026-10-24-35, from M5.1 on; wording from Economy's `scratch/m51-econ/ac22/g4/bb10-gate-note.md`).** For every integer sim second while 10 ≤ Farm < maxLevel, the second is *affordable* if some `next-goal` `candidates()` row has coins ≥ cost **and** is meaningful: `optionGain(s, b, key).gain > 0` (`econ/best-buy.ts`, `incomePerSec` with `assumeWorking`), or the row is FARM, W1–W3 or W4/Grader (the BBv2-1 exemption; HoP to confirm this reading). Zero-gain rows never make a second affordable. G4 = the longest contiguous run of non-affordable seconds per seed, from F10 to maxLevel; P50/P95 by `tests/pacing-guards` `quantile`, seeds 1–200. Hatches never count as affordable below MAX (BR-AC36). Clauses unchanged from D-HOP-2026-10-24-30: (1) worst seed ≤ the same-seeds no-mechanics baseline (blocking); (2) P95 ≤ 180 s (blocking); (3) worst seed ≤ 180 s (warning). The any-row G4 is not used on the BBv2 bot: it degenerates to ≈ 0 s (P95 2 s) because the zero-gain Collection row stays affordable. Reproduced by both PMFU (`farm/scratch/e10/`) and Economy (`scratch/m51-econ/ac22/g4/`): BBv2 no-hatch **116 / 199 / 226**, BBv2 + Farm-first **114 / 197 / 231**. Strict gain > 0 without the exemption (282 / 357 / 443) is not usable, because Farm steps read as zero immediate gain.
- **Direction check:** M5.0's active stack already moves L30 29–35% sooner on the active path (`research/v2-slices/economy.md` §0 item 4). A ~17% faster ★ path goes the same way and needs no cost payback.

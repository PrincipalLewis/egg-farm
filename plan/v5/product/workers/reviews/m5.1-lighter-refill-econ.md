# M5.1 lighter Refill: Economy review (C2 auto-refill + D auto-rest)
2026-10-24 20:10 CEST (updated 22:25: seller15 reserve 0, Asleep-waiver M5-G1) · Economy/Balance · Source: `product/workers/m5.1-lighter-refill-proposal.md` v0.1.1 (sha256 cb4c2ed5…) · Status: research only (D-CEO-20261024-05). No CSV, code, commit or PR. `product/economy.csv` stays c7bb783f.

## 0. Verdict
- **The window gate passes, but only if the reserve is 0 or the idle comparator keeps manual refills.** With `tc_autorefill_reserve` 1 and an idle bot that uses auto-refill, the worst seed is 2.44 (optimal) and 2.49 (casual), which **FAILS** ≤ 2.25. With reserve 0 the worst seed is 1.963 / 1.942, a PASS.
- **Recommended values:**
  - `tc_autorefill_at_pct` **20**
  - `tc_autorefill_reserve` **0** (changed from the proposed 1)
  - `tc_autorest_on_empty` **1**
  - `tc_ability_energy_cost` **10** (unchanged)
  - `m4.flags.autoRefill` default **0**
  - `tc_lab_drain_cut` (M5.2) is not modelled here. Hold it to ≤ 0.05/level unless regen is retuned (§5).
- **C2 lightens taps without changing pacing.**
  - An auto-only player makes 0 manual Refill taps; a manual player makes 12.0 per 30 min. Auto spends 10.1–10.7 charges per 30 min for them.
  - Auto-only income is **96.3–96.7%** of best manual play over F10–29 (median same-state window 0.954), so manual stays the better path.
  - L10, E-G4 P95 and the active premium are unchanged within ±1 pp.
- **D (auto-Rest) has no economic effect.** It recorded 0 s of Rest in every tab-open run, because a charge (regen 150 s) always comes back before Rest gains anything. It is a dead-end/UX feature only, and it does not reduce `mh_asleep` stalls in these sims.
- **AC-SC8 (≥ 20% of L10–17 charge-starved) fails before C2.** The M5.1 base (rc2 + call cost 10, manual) measures 0.3–0.7%. C2 raises it to 1.4–7.0%. On `scratch/v2-econ/economy.m50-draft.csv` (sha256 8dc90e2b…) with the M5.0 stack, L10–17 lasts about 6 min, so it can't be the C2 gate (open question Q-HoP-2).

## 3. Answers to the proposal's econ questions
1. **`tc_autorefill_at_pct` (10–30):**

   | at_pct | Auto/manual income | Charges per 30 min | Market Hand stall P95 | E-G4 P95 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 10 | 95.0 / 94.8% | 9.4 / 9.2 | 8.4 / 4.4 s | 171 / 156 |
   | **20** | **96.4 / 96.7 / 96.3%** | 10.4 / 10.3 / 10.1 | 0 | 166 / 154 / 139 |
   | 30 | 97.4 / 97.3% | 11.4 / 11.3 | 0 | 148 / 141 |

   Income is over F10–29 for optimal / seller15 (/ casual); 10 and 30 are on seeds 1–100. **Recommend 20.** It is the lowest value with no sales stalls, and it keeps the Tired cost visible (about 90 s of Tired per cycle).
2. **`tc_autorefill_reserve` (0–1): recommend 0.**
   - Reserve 1 fails M5-G1 when the idle player uses the toggle (worst seed 2.44 / 2.49).
   - Reserve 0 passes (1.963 / 1.942), with auto/manual income of 97.1 / 96.9%.
   - The "keep a charge for my own pick" goal still holds, because a manual tap pre-empts auto (§5.6 of the proposal). Any player who wants a reserve can leave a worker off auto.
   - If PM Workers wants to keep 1, the reserve must be waived for an Asleep worker (WAIVE: income 95.8 / 96.0%, asleep 0). WAIVE + reserve 1 against idle-on-auto (`out/wi-waive1.json`): optimal 1.883 / 2.049 and casual 1.794 / 2.004 both **pass**, with less headroom than reserve 0. Both options are now legal; reserve 0 is still preferred.
3. **`tc_autorest_on_empty`: 1 is fine.** It is economically neutral (0 s Rest in every tab-open run, and REST=0 gives the same results as REST=1). It never fires offline, because energy and charges are frozen. With reserve 1, an Asleep worker holding 1 charge is neither refilled nor resting: a dead end that reserve 0 removes.
4. **`tc_ability_energy_cost` 10, modelled with C2 on:** M5-G1 is identical to M5.0 (1.936). Premium and L10 are unchanged with the frenzy-holder policy. Keep 10.
5. **PM's gate "auto-only income 85–95% of best manual":** measured **96.3–96.7%** at 20/1 and about 97% at 20/0. Manual stays better on 95% of windows (P95 auto/manual = 1.00; worst window 1.03–1.09, from seeds where the manual bot spent a charge early). To land inside 95% you would need at_pct 10, which brings Market Hand stalls. **Recommend widening the band to 85–97%.**
6. **AC-SC8 (≥ 20% of L10–17 charge-starved):** with C2 on it is **1.4–7.0% (FAIL)**, but the flag-off M5.1 base already fails at 0.3–0.7%. Demand at the 32% refill point is about 0.38/min against a supply of 0.40/min. The rc2 30% figure was measured on the pre-M4 curve. C2 doesn't erode scarcity; it raises the starved share because auto banks charges less efficiently.
7. **`tc_lab_drain_cut` (M5.2), not simulated:** with −10%/level × 3, demand falls to about 0.27–0.34/min against 0.40/min supply, so charge scarcity is gone. Pair it with regen 150 → 180–200 s, or cap it at 0.05/level. Re-run when the Lab framework exists.

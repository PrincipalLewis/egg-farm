## 0. Verdict (headline)
**"Asleep / Tired / Unpaid < Working at every cell" FAILS today, both with and without autoRefill, and under Pay wage.** No worker-tunable value fixes it. The cause is a redundant worker; the fix is a rule (§4, plus §5.3 if Pay wage ships).

### 2.3 Unpaid, explicitly
- In the sim Unpaid ≡ Asleep for output (no action, no wage, `workers.ts:8`), and it **does not drain**. So the Unpaid breach cells and margins are the §2 Asleep cells and margins, for both the Market Hand and the Coop Hand. Hawker and Grader draw no wage, so they can't be Unpaid.
- **Today (auto-resume):** Unpaid can't be held by choice. The worker resumes as soon as coins ≥ 1 min of wage plus the reserve (`tick.ts:62–67`), so the breach is transient.
- **The §4 clock-wage rule doesn't touch Unpaid.** It charges only `asleep`. Under auto-resume that is harmless, because Unpaid ends once coins allow.
- **Under explicit Pay wage (no auto-resume), Unpaid becomes a holdable free state.** A player who drops below one wage interval once, then never presses Pay, keeps a redundant worker Unpaid for free. With or without the clock rule, that out-earns Working by the full wage in every §2 breach cell (§5).

## 3. Why no worker-tunable fix exists (PM Workers' constraint 1)
In every out-earning cell, net(Asleep) − net(Working) equals the wage to the cent: W1 = 0.25·F $/min, W2 = 0.5·F $/min (map §2). The working worker's marginal sales are exactly 0, because the player's own taps or check-ins already move every egg.
- **Wage (`w1_wage_per_min`, `w2_wage_per_min`):** any wage > 0 keeps the breach. Wage 0 gives a tie, not Asleep < Working, and gives away about $0.06–0.37/s per worker in every other cell.
- **Drain (`drain_s_per_pct`):** changes how often a worker sleeps, not the per-cell sign.
- **Tired multipliers (`w1/w2_tired_every_s`, `w3_tired_demand_mult`, `w4_tired_bonus_share`):** cut output only where output is marginal. In the redundant cells Tired is a tie, never a gain. Tired never out-earns Working anywhere (worst −$0.03/s, a rounding artefact).
- **`w2_asleep_collect_share` (0.15 canonical, effective 0):** raising it makes Asleep earn more, which is the wrong direction. Keep it unread (0) or ≤ 0.15.
- **So the fix needs a rule.** The smallest rule found is §4. One more limit: even with a rule, Asleep is strictly worse than Working only where the worker has non-redundant work. Where the player does the job by hand, the best possible result is a tie.
  - At F10+, the M5.0 calls give every working worker a non-redundant bonus (Rush ×1.15, Sweep A lay ×1.15, Shout, Inspect, about 1.5% of sales each, MODEL). That bonus is at least 8× the wage (F12: about $0.8–1.7/s against $0.05–0.10/s), so for players who use calls, Asleep < Working holds strictly at F10+.

## 4. Proposal: "wages run on the clock" (Asleep and Resting workers keep drawing their wage)
- **Rule:** a hired Market Hand or Coop Hand that is Asleep (0%), or Resting under the lighter-refill D add-on, is charged its wage on the same `every_s` schedule as when Working, at the same `w*_wage_per_min`. Unpaid and Paused are unchanged.
  - No new column. It reuses the existing wage tunables, with one line in `tick.ts:73` (scratch implementation in `h2/src/sim/tick.ts`, flag `__clockWage`).
  - It lands in the M5.1 L0–30 unfreeze (D-CEO-08).
- **Map with the rule (`out/mapclock.json`, F3–30, C step 6, seed 1, all profiles):**
  - **0 cells** where Asleep out-earns Working, for any worker or profile.
  - Hawker shows 7–10 cells at −$0.0003/s, which is cent rounding on Grader-multiplied sales; it has no wage.
  - Ties remain exactly in the redundant cells: Market Hand 156/168, Coop Hand 110–120/144 for tappers, Hawker 126–130/138.
  - Tired never out-earns Working.

### 5.3 Fix candidate for Pay wage: a held-Unpaid worker is billed whenever coins allow
- **Rule (scratch flag `__clockUnpaid`, `h2/src/sim/tick.ts:74`):** this extends §4. A hired Market Hand or Coop Hand that is Unpaid is charged its normal wage on its `every_s` schedule whenever coins cover it. Pressing Pay only restarts the work. A player who is genuinely broke is still not charged.
- **Map (`CLOCK=1 CLOCKU=1`, 200 seeds, ac7 + optimal, C 3/6/9 → `out/valclocku-F*.json`):**

## 6. Decisions needed
**PM Workers**
- **Q-WF-1, strict "<" isn't reachable with worker tunables.** Wherever the player already does a worker's job (their own taps cover the Coop Hand, check-ins every ≤ 30 s cover the Market Hand, or the Hawker is supply-bound), every failure state can at best tie Working. That holds even under the §4 and §5.3 rules. Two options:
  - accept "≤ everywhere, < wherever the worker has non-redundant work" as the AC; or
  - give a Working worker a small non-redundant output. At F10+ the M5.0 calls already do this.
- **Q-WF-2:** adopt the §4 clock-wage rule (Asleep and Resting keep drawing the wage)? It's one line, adds no column, and its gate Δ is 0.
- **Q-WF-3, Pay wage vs auto-resume:** keep auto-resume, where Unpaid can't be held, or ship explicit Pay? Pay is only safe together with §5.3 (bill a held-Unpaid worker when coins allow). Without §5.3, Unpaid out-earns Working by the full wage in every redundant cell, with or without the clock rule.

**Head of Product**
- **Q-WF-4:** the clock-wage rule (+ §5.3 if Pay ships) changes L0–30 worker behaviour. It belongs in the M5.1 L0–30 unfreeze (D-CEO-08), so it needs your sign-off on scope there. No CSV change is needed.

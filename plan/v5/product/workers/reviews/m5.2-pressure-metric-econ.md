- **Why new runs were wanted:** the existing per-seed dumps (`scratch/m51-ship/out/ps-at30.*`, `ps-clk1.base.*`) hold only the per-seed share, and `trace-raw.json` covers only 3 cells. Worker-hours and rolling windows need per-second series.
- **Harness:** `scratch/m51-refill/h2/m5p.ts` = `m5s.ts` + a per-second series from F ≥ 10 (starved ticks, excess Refillable workers over charges, eligible workers) + an optional one-charge perturbation. Driver `h2/m5px.ts`; runner `h2/press-run.sh` (each run starts only when fewer than 2 sims are running box-wide); analysis `scratch/m52-press/press.py`. With `pert` 0, play is identical to `m5s.ts`, and the series reproduces the harness share to ≤ 0.0003 (smoke, casual s116–118).
- **Metrics (per seed):**
  - **M0, current:** charge-starved share over F10–29 = starved ticks ÷ ticks at 10 ≤ F < 30 (LR-G3; AC-LR10).
  - **M1, starved worker-seconds per worker-hour, F10–29:** Σ max(0, Refillable − charges) × dt ÷ (Σ eligible workers × dt ÷ 3600). Eligible = hired, not Paused, not Unpaid. Unit: s per worker-hour.
  - **M1b, starved seconds per worker-hour (binary), F10–29:** starved s ÷ worker-hours. This is M0 × 3600 ÷ (mean eligible workers), shown because it is a literal reading of PMW's name.
  - **M2, full session:** M1 and M0 from L10 to the end of the 12 000 s session (including the F30 dwell). Note: "L10–30" read literally is the M0/M1 window itself; M2 is the longer reading.
  - **M3, rolling 30 min:** M1 and M0 over 1 800 s windows stepped every 60 s from L10 to the session end; per seed, the **max** and the **mean** over windows.
- **±1 refill sensitivity:** a direct perturbation in the harness. At the first tick at F ≥ 20, one charge is **granted** (+1, if below the cap; otherwise at the first tick it is below) or **removed** (−1, if one is held; otherwise at the first tick one is held). That is exactly one refill's worth of charge, inserted or removed at a fixed point. The bots' own refill logic then decides what the energy does, so the trajectory is allowed to diverge, as it would in play. Change = |metric(pert) − metric(base)| per seed, reported as median and P95, ÷ the base P50.

## 2. Measured at 200 seeds: pooled time-weighted share (PMW scope)
**What the pooled share is.** Pooled share = Σ over seeds of charge-starved seconds ÷ Σ over seeds of seconds, with each second classified on the LR-G3 rule (Refillable workers > charges held), over F10–29, at a 1 s resolution from the 0.1 s sim. It is a **population** figure with per-second starvation over F10–29. It is **not the AC-LR10 harness median** (the median of per-seed shares). **On the same CSV, pooled and median are about level** (corrected 2026-10-25; the `d4e47f55` text compared 8dc90e2b pooled with o1a medians, which is cross-CSV). On 8dc90e2b, pooled on is 35.5 / 35.9 / 34.9% vs median 36.5 / 35.7 / 35.0%, and pooled off is 22.3 / 23.7 / 25.7% vs median 23.1 / 24.8 / 26.6%. On o1a (§5), pooled on is 33.1 / 32.4 / 30.7% vs median 33.5 / 32.7 / 30.5%, and pooled off is 15.2 / 17.1 / 19.4% vs median 16.1 / 18.1 / 20.4%. So pooled on is within ±1 pp of the median, and pooled off runs about 1 pp lower. Optimal / seller15 / casual.

- **Per seed, one charge matters more than PMW's 5–10 pp, and it is asymmetric.** +1 moves a seed's share by ~0 at the median (P95 6–15 pp). −1 adds a **per-seed median of +18.3 / +18.2 / +20.4 pp on** (optimal / seller15 / casual; off +21.8 / +19.7 / +19.0 pp), with P95 |Δ| 30–37 pp. This is real dynamics, not a counting fault. I checked casual s1–s3: removing a charge while the pool sits at the cap switches the seed from "cap-saturated, regen wasted" to "regen-bound". ## 4. Recommendation (suggestion only; PMW drafts the spec, HoP sets bars)
- **The pooled-share recommendation holds.** The pooled F10–29 share is steady at 200 seeds (SE ≈ 0.5 pp on, 0.6 pp off; ratio SE 0.03–0.04), and one seed's charge-timing luck moves it by < 1 SE. It separates on from off by 9–13 pp (15–25 SE; ratio 1.36–1.59). It also responds strongly to a real supply change (a systematic −1 charge moves the pooled on share +17.5 to +20.7 pp), which is what a scarcity metric should do.
- **It strengthens the case against a per-seed absolute floor:** a single charge moves one seed by a median +18.2 to +20.4 pp (on), so a hard per-seed floor (like AC-LR10 (ii)) will keep catching charge-timing seeds like s148 / s116.
- **Candidate bar shape:**
  - (a) per profile, pooled on share ≥ X, with X from the measured 34.9–35.9% less a margin (e.g. ≥ 30%);
  - (b) pooled on ÷ off ≥ Y, measured 1.36–1.59 (e.g. Y ≈ 1.2), so auto must not erase scarcity relative to manual;
  - (c) at most a distributional per-seed guard. Measured on-share P5 is 23.5–25.6% and P10 26.4–27.1%; (on − off) P5 is −3.2…+1.3 pp. A guard like "on-share P5 ≥ 20%" would pass with margin.
- **Caveat:** these are 8dc90e2b runs. If the bar is set on o1a, re-pool on o1a; it is one 18-run batch with the same scripts and no code change.

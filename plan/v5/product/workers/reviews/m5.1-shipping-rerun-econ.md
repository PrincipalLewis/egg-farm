## 4. FC-G7: a "non-redundant" definition and tie tolerance for AC-FC6 (item 4)
Data: the 3-seed map `scratch/m51-refill/out/map-{a,b}.json` (3,564 cells, excluding away Market Hand per Econ-FC §2.1), plus the 200-seed validation cells `val200-F*.json`, `val200c-F*.json` and `valunp*-F*.json` (24 cells each).

**Gross marginal output** G(w, cell) = net(Working) − net(Asleep, clock wage on). The same wage is charged in both, so G is exactly the sales the Working worker adds.
- Today's data gives G = mAsleep + wage (W1 F/240 $/s, W2 F/120 $/s, W3/W4 0).
- PMW's "net(Working) − net(Asleep, wage zeroed) > 0" means the same thing as long as "wage zeroed" removes the wage from both sides. Please word it as above to avoid that ambiguity.

**What the data shows:**
- **3-seed map |G|, cells per band:** 0 → 1,313; < $0.001/s → 1,001; $0.001–0.01 → 68; $0.01–0.1 → 18; $0.1–1 → 1; $1–10 → 7; ≥ $10 → 1,156.
  - Every value below $0.1/s is noise:
    - wage-interval phase at the window edge, up to $0.034/s (F20 Coop Hand ac7, identical across C);
    - cent rounding on Grader-multiplied sales.
  - The smallest real G is $0.16/s (F14 C3 Coop Hand, ac7). The next is $1.6/s.
- **200 seeds:** the largest |G| in a redundant cell is **$0.0024/s** (F18 Market Hand, 5.6 × 10⁻⁶ of net). The smallest real G is **$0.367/s** (F12 C3 Coop Hand, 0.33% of net). Tired's worst Δ is −$0.0005/s.

**Proposed definitions** (judged on 200-seed cell means, MEAS 300 s after a 120 s warm-up, common random numbers):
- **Tie tolerance τ = $0.005/s per cell**, applied as |net(state) − net(Working)| ≤ τ. That is twice the largest 200-seed noise ($0.0024) and 70× below the smallest real G. A relative form isn't needed: the noise doesn't scale with income (5.6 × 10⁻⁶ at F18), and a relative τ of 10⁻⁴ would swallow real effects at F30 (Tired −$0.025/s at F30 C6).
- **Non-redundant cell for worker w:** G(w, cell) > **$0.05/s**, i.e. 10 τ, and above every noise value seen on 200 seeds.
- **AC-FC6 then reads:**
  - every state ≤ Working + τ in every cell;
  - Asleep < Working − τ in every non-redundant cell.

**Two states need a ruling, because the strict clause can't apply to them as written:**
- **Tired (partial output):** Tired can tie Working even where the worker is non-redundant, because a 4 s cadence still covers the work. Example: F12 C3 Coop Hand, ac7: G = $0.367/s, but Tired − Working = −$0.0005/s, a tie.
  - **Proposal:** Tired is held to "≤ Working + τ" everywhere. Strict < applies to Tired only where Tired actually lowers output (net(Working) − net(Tired) > τ).
  - On the 200-seed cells Tired is never above Working + τ. Where Tired does cost, it costs $0.025–361/s.
- **Unpaid:** auto-resume stays (D-HOP-45), so Unpaid can't be held and the AC needs a measurable form.
  - A *held* Unpaid worker (no wage, no output, no clock billing; `valunp*`) equals today's Asleep to the cent. It sits at Working − G + wage, so it beats Working + τ in every redundant cell, by the wage ($0.029–0.25/s).
  - **Proposal:** take Unpaid out of the held-cell map and test it as a transient: share of time Unpaid and Asleep↔Unpaid toggles, §3.
  - If HoP wants Unpaid in the held map, it fails "≤" in redundant cells. Only billing a held Unpaid worker (Econ-FC §5.3, moot since Pay wage closed) removes that.

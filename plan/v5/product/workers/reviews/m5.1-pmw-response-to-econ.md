## 1. Q-WF-3 — Pay wage vs auto-resume → **Keep auto-resume. Do not ship Pay wage in M5.1; close the parked item.** [HoP approval]
- **No economic upside.** A prompt payer is byte-for-byte the auto-resume economy: G4, L10 and income identical on all three profiles, with and without autoRefill and with the clock rule (Econ-FC §5.2, `fn-pay.json`, `fn-payclock.json`). Pay wage changes "only how long a player can stay in a failure state" (Econ-FC §5.2).
- **Without §5.3 it is an exploit.** A held-Unpaid worker equals Asleep to the cent in 24/24 cells and out-earns Working by the full wage, indefinitely; the clock rule doesn't close it (5 of 6 F12 cells still out-earn) (Econ-FC §5.1). That is the "neglect pays" failure we're fixing.
- **With §5.3 it's legible only badly.** §5.3 (0/48 cells out-earn, Econ-FC §5.3) bills a worker who isn't working and still makes the player tap Pay to restart. To the player: "I'm being charged *and* I have to pay to get them back." That is a punishing double-feel and a confusing model (what does "Pay" buy if I'm already paying?). Auto-resume + clock wage gives the same economics with one consistent rule: *hired workers are paid; if you can't pay, they stop and come back automatically when you can.*
- **Workers best practice.** Unpaid today is telegraphed and recoverable with no chore: "Resumes at $X" caption + aria, next patch (rc1-asleep v1.0.9–v1.0.11; D-HOP-32 follow-up 18:55). Pay wage adds a tap-chore in the same release whose goal is fewer chore taps (lighter refill). Pausing remains the free, deliberate way to stop a wage (pause spec, "Accepted, not an exploit").
- **Cost avoided:** a new button/state, §5.3 rule, extra ACs, UI at 360 in an already tight card row (refill v0.1.1 §8 note on the action row).
- **If HoP still wants Pay:** it ships only with §5.3 (ruled), and needs copy that says the bill runs while Unpaid ("Unpaid · billed $X/min · Pay to restart"), which PMW considers worse than today's caption.

## 3. Q-PMW-4 — Is D (auto-Rest) worth 0.5 dd for UX alone? → **No. Cut D from M5.1; save 0.5 dd.** [HoP approval]
- **No economic effect:** 0 s of Rest in every tab-open run; REST=0 gives the same results as REST=1 (Econ-LR §0, §2.5, §3.3). It never fires offline (Econ-LR §3.3).
- **The dead end it was for is gone with reserve 0.** The dead end was "Asleep worker holding 1 charge, neither refilled nor resting", which "reserve 0 removes" (Econ-LR §3.3). With reserve 0 an asleep worker on auto takes the next charge within one regen (150 s, Econ-LR §1), versus Rest's 0→100 in 60 min (rc2 AC-RF6).
- **It now hurts legibility.** Under the clock-wage ruling a Resting worker is paid, while Pause (also "rest" to the player) is "On break · no wage". Two rest-like states with opposite wage rules, one of them automatic, is exactly the hidden-drain confusion the running-wage ruling wants to avoid.
- It doesn't reduce `mh_asleep` stalls in sims (Econ-LR §0).

## 4. Auto/manual income band (Q-PMW-2) → **Widen to 85–97%, judged at the shipping values (20 / reserve 0), with a dominance guard: manual ≥ auto on ≥ 95% of windows (P95 auto/manual ≤ 1.00).** [HoP approval; one Econ confirmation first]

- Getting under 95% needs at_pct 10, which brings Market Hand stalls (P95 8.4 / 4.4 s, Econ-LR §3.1) — a worse player outcome than a slightly generous auto. 20 also keeps a visible Tired cost (~90 s Tired per cycle, Econ-LR §3.1), so auto is felt, not free.
- Best practice: auto must be comfortable (not punished) and manual must still win; the window-dominance guard expresses "manual wins" directly, which the % band only approximates.
- **Conflict to resolve:** Econ-LR §3.2's reserve-0 optimal figure 97.1% is above Econ's own proposed 97% cap. **Econ: please confirm the reserve-0 auto/manual figure on seeds 1–200** (state whether §3.2 is the 100-seed sweep). If optimal is > 97.0% on 200 seeds, PMW proposes keeping the 97% cap and letting the dominance guard decide, and brings the choice (cap vs nudge at_pct) to HoP rather than inventing a new cap.

## 1. Problem and constraint
- Drain 9 s per 1% (100→0 in 15 min). Refill (rc2 v1.2) restores 1 worker to 100% from ≤50%, costs 1 charge; charges regen 150 s, cap 2 (0.40/min). Normal demand 0.33–0.44/min, so the chore is ≈1 tap per worker per 7.5–15 min, forever.
- M5.1 adds a 10-energy cost per call (5-min cooldown): +90 s of drain per 300 s, ≈+30% drain on a calling worker → demand rises to ≈0.43–0.57/min.
- **Constraint:** lighten the *taps*, not the *charges*. Supply < demand must still hold. ~~rc2 AC-SC8 (≥20% of L10–17 charge-starved)~~ is **retired** (D-HOP-2026-10-24-44 Q-HoP-2); its replacement is AC-LR10 (auto-refill must not erase scarcity). Anything that adds energy for free erases the scarcity we just bought.

## 5. Edge cases
1. Offline / hidden ≥ `offline.offlineMinSeconds` (60 s, balance.json): energy, charges and regen are frozen (rc2; HoE ruling 3: sim ticks only, no catch-up, load clamps), so auto never fires offline; it evaluates on the first tick back. Hidden < 60 s runs live.
2. Paused worker: excluded from auto (paused ≠ tired, no drain under Pause, spec v1.3.5). Unpaid: excluded (a refill doesn't fix wages). Asleep: **included** (with reserve 0 an asleep worker takes the next charge; no dead end). Asleep workers draw their wage (clock wage, failure-cost FC-R1). Phase per U1 (§5a): the worker keeps the same `cdS` across Working, Tired and Asleep, and an auto or manual Refill does not reset it (AC-LR20).
3. A call's 10-energy cost drops a worker below `tc_autorefill_at_pct` (A; 30% at the shipping value): auto fires on the next sim tick, after the call starts; the call is never blocked or delayed by auto.
4. Two workers cross on the same tick with 1 spendable charge: costliest `workerAtRisk` first; tie → rc2 AC-RF3 order (lower energy first, then W1→W4). v0.4 aligns the old "lower slot index" tie-break with rc2 so the sheet's top row and auto's pick never differ.
5. Menus open: the sim advances under menus (RC1 `Game.frame()`), so auto may fire under a sheet; the card and charge count update live, toast only after the menu closes (one coalesced toast, AC-LR14: "Auto-refilled Market Hand" / "Auto-refilled N workers").
6. Manual tap and auto on the same tick: manual wins; auto re-evaluates with the new charge count.
7. Toggle on a worker already ≤ `tc_autorefill_at_pct` (≤ 30% at the shipping value): fires on the next tick if a charge is spendable.
8. Save: `autoRefill` per worker, optional field, default false, no version bump.
9. Flag `m4.flags.autoRefill` in balance.json, default 0; off = M5.1 rc2 behaviour exactly. It follows the existing `m4.flags.*` convention (`m4.flags.abilities`, `m4.flags.pause`, HoE §11 / D-C1) unless HoE says otherwise. **Requires the rc2 Refill flag `m4.flags.rc2Refill` = 1** (LR-N3; CONFIRMED by HoE, engineering/decisions.md; failure-cost v0.3.6 FC-R9). With `m4.flags.rc2Refill` = 0 the Auto-refill row is not rendered and the auto-refill logic does not run, whatever `m4.flags.autoRefill` says (there are no charges and the pre-rc2 Manage cooldown path never meets auto). AC-LR19.

- **Term:** U2's *auto-resume reserve* is a coin reserve in the Unpaid resume check. It is unrelated to the charge reserve `tc_autorefill_reserve` (0, §4), which U2 does not touch.
- **Timing:** the "next tick" in U2 falls in the same sim step as the resume in the reference src (failure-cost §2a note 2).

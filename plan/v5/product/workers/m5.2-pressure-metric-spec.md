## 2. Rationale
- AC-LR10's per-seed bars are fragile: the (ii) floor moved 15% → 12% (D-HOP-2026-10-25-05) and sits at +0.1 pp on casual o1a (s148, K-W3). Cause: charge timing on a short ~22–28 min F10–29 stretch, not a defect.
- **Per seed, one charge is a cliff** (Econ-PM §2.3, 8dc90e2b): removing one charge at F20 adds a per-seed median of +18.3 / +18.2 / +20.4 pp on (off +21.8 / +19.7 / +19.0; P95 |Δ| 30–37 pp). Real game behaviour (regen-bound until F30).
- **Pooled, the same luck is noise:** one perturbed seed moves the pooled share ≤ 0.23 pp and the ratio ≤ 0.015 (8dc90e2b); on o1a the +1 single-seed ratio moves are ≤ 0.004 on / ≤ 0.012 off.
- **Why a difference, not an absolute floor (D-HOP-2026-10-25-15):** the absolute pooled on-share fell 2.4–4.2 pp from 8dc90e2b to o1a, leaving casual 1.6 SE over 30% and under it at on +1. The pooled on − off difference measures what pressure means here (auto-on has more charge pressure than best-manual play), is insensitive to baseline shifts that move both arms, and casual clears by ≥ 7.5 SE (≥ 3.9 SE at on +1).
- **Absolute floors kept:** a difference cannot see a supply increase applied to both arms (both +1 widens it to 18.70 / 16.44 / 12.36 pp). **PM3 (per-seed on-share P5 ≥ 20%) and AC-LR10 (on median ≥ 30%, P5 ≥ 20%, per-seed (i)/(ii)) are the absolute floors that catch a supply increase on both sides.**
- Rejected shapes: worker-hour metrics; full-session and rolling 30-min windows (F30 dwell; d 0.32–0.74 vs 1.08–1.59) (Econ-PM §1, §3).

## 10. Final AC set (for HoQA; self-contained)
### 10.1 Definitions
- **Tick:** 0.1 s sim step. **Charge-starved tick** (LR-G3, LR v0.8.8 §6 line 53): the number of workers that are hired, not Paused, not Unpaid, and at energy ≤ economy.csv `refill_threshold_pct` (50; Asleep included) is greater than the charges held. Offline and hidden ≥ 60 s excluded (none in the sim).
- **Window W (per seed):** from the first second at farm level F ≥ 10 up to, not including, the second in which F reaches 30 (10 ≤ F < 30). In Econ's output: seconds `0 … K−1` of the per-second series, K = min(len, ceil(t30) − 1 − start).
- **Tick accounting (P2, HoQA wording):** Second index k = ceil(t) − 1 − start for the tick ending at sim time t; start = the second of the first tick at F ≥ 10. **Ticks in W = 10 × K** (whole seconds; the first partial second counts as 10). If a seed never reaches F10 in an arm, the run is reported to Econ and HoP before a verdict (0 / 1,200 on o1a). If F30 is never reached, W runs to session end (0 / 1,200 on o1a). (Counting the first partial second as 10 ticks biases each seed's denominator by ≤ 0.9 s; o1a K = 1,139–1,504 s.)
- **Per-seed share** s_i = starved ticks in W ÷ (10 × K) (= the AC-LR10 metric).
- **Pooled share** P = Σ_i starved ticks in W_i ÷ Σ_i (10 × K_i) (ratio of sums, not the mean of per-seed shares), over seeds 1–200, per profile and arm.
- **Arms (paired, same seed):** *on* = flag-on, auto-refill on for every worker, `tc_autorefill_at_pct` 30, `tc_autorefill_reserve` 0, zero manual refills; *off* = flag-off best-manual bot (refills at ≤ 32% at its check-ins, or ≤ 50% when charges are at the cap; `MANAT=32`). Both: B3 shipping settings, auto-rest off, clock wage on, `charge_regen_s` 150, `charge_cap` 2, no perturbation (`PERT` 0).
- **Session stack (P1, HoQA wording):** Both arms also use the M5.0 stack of `h2/m5px.ts` (sha1 c20ad937), lines 8–10:
  - `__clockWage` on, `__payWage` off;
  - streakCap 0.28, tapShare 0.03;
  - Broody on; golden gap 120–300 s; abilities on;
  - sweep A ×1.15;
  - hold (frenzy-holder) on, warm on, ssZero on;
  - RC2 call cost 10, manual `manAt` 32 (off) / auto {at 30, reserve 0, rest off} (on);
  - 12,000 s per session.

  A replacement harness must match every item. (PMW note, read from the same lines: the stack also sets `pause: false` and auto `waive0: false`; a replacement must match those too.)
- **D** = P_on − P_off (percentage points). **R** = P_on ÷ P_off.
- **P5** = harness nearest-rank 5th percentile: sort the 200 values ascending, take element ceil(0.05 × 200) = 10th (1-based).

### 10.4 SE method
- **Econ (P_on, P_off, R):** bootstrap over seeds, 2 000 resamples, seeds drawn with replacement and used for both arms together (paired), SE = sample SD of the resampled statistic.
- **P5 (P4, HoQA wording):** P5: the same paired seed bootstrap applied to the nearest-rank P5 of s_i(on). QA o1a values: 1.09 / 1.24 / 0.65 pp.
- **D:** report both, labelled. *Paired*: the same bootstrap applied to D (preferred). *Independent*: √(SE_on² + SE_off²), assumes independence (conservative here).
- SE is for reporting and the port parity check (§10.2 (ii)); the gate decision uses the point values in 10.2.

# M4.2: Worker fatigue visibility (RV-3, D-HOP-36). PM, Workers, v0.2.5 (v0.2.5 2026-10-30 14:30 CET: copy erratum, no AC/number/flag change, L98 Hawker effect "fewer customers" → "sales slowed" per Art ffb13c19 / art-ux/decisions.md L560, D-PMW-2026-10-30-29; v0.2.4 2026-10-30 14:29 CET: dated erratum, no AC/number/flag change, L99 Egg Grader {pct} formula aligned to code, D-PMW-2026-10-30-28; v0.2 approved with edits, D-HOP-37, Oct 19 2026; v0.2.3 2026-10-30 14:20 CET: post-freeze dated erratum, no AC/number/flag change, Rule 5 L29 pointer to failure-cost AC-FC9, D-PMW-2026-10-30-25)
Sources:
- playtest/player-notes/2026-10-19-session-m4b.md: at about 30 min every worker was Tired (12–19%) and income was flat at $20–30/s. After Manage all it rose to about $75/s, and this repeated every ~10 min with no cue.
- critic/review-m4b-dd223f5-2026-10-19.md: the goal bubble never mentions sleeping workers, asleep workers sit below the fold, Manage all moves under the cursor, and asleep workers are never explained.

**Goal.** A player always knows when fatigue is costing them money, how much, and the one tap that fixes it. This keeps failure *telegraphed and recoverable* rather than a silent slowdown that reads as "the game got slow".
**Non-goals.** No fatigue, wage or energy tuning (P41-6 guardrail: no L0–11 change). No new mechanics.
**Reuse.** If any of these aren't on the M4 branch, port them; don't redesign:
- rc1-asleep-state-spec (Asleep and Unpaid banners, nav badge, AC-R1–R9)
- the rc1 T5 fix c231c02 (Refill bar from 1 tired worker)
- the Cut A v3 rate ruling (real incomePerSec, banners naming role and effect)
- rc2 Shift sheet stability (re-sort on open or every 5 s, never within 1 s of a tap)

## Rules
1. **Energy state cue on every worker surface.** The thresholds come from data: yawn_at_pct=50, Tired below 30%, Asleep at 0.
   - Labels and icons: Refillable 30–50% (yawn), Tired 1–29% (droop plus amber), Asleep 0% (Zzz plus red).
   - These appear on the worker card, on the worker's sprite in the farm scene (Art & UX), and on the Workers tab badge.
   - The colour always comes with an icon and text, never colour alone.
2. **Fatigue cost chip on the HUD rate.**
   - When fatigueLoss = Σ workerAtRisk over Tired and Asleep workers is at least fatigue_cue_min_share (0.15) of incomePerSec plus fatigueLoss, sustained for fatigue_cue_hold_s (5 s), show a chip next to the top-bar rate reading "⚠ Tired workers −$X/s".
   - Tapping the chip opens the Workers tab or Shift sheet, scrolled so the costliest worker is first.
   - The chip clears within 1 s once the share drops below the threshold.
   - It's computed with the same incomePerSec selector the HUD uses.
3. **Asleep on the farm screen.** Whenever any worker is Asleep, the rc1 Asleep banner shows on the farm screen, not only in Workers. It names the role and its real effect (Cut A v3 ruling) and carries a Refill action.
4. **Goal bubble.** When fatigueLoss ≥ fatigue_cue_min_share, the bubble shows "Refill workers: +$X/s" ahead of buy goals. This is the same signal as the chip, and it stays until the condition clears, so the bubble doesn't flip-flop. When the coop is full, the coop-full hint keeps its priority (coop-full > fatigue > buy goals > M4 hints).
5. **One-time explanation.** The first time any worker crosses into Tired (or Asleep, if that comes first), show a non-blocking coach card once per save. Placeholder copy, final strings from Art & UX:
   - "Workers tire as they work. Tired workers earn less, and asleep ones stop. Tap Refill to wake them."
   - It's anchored above the tab bar and never covers it (the session-3 finding). A Got it button closes it, and it also auto-closes after 8 s.
   - The first Asleep event later gets a one-line follow-up toast, again once per save. *[Pointer 14:20 CET, D-PMW-2026-10-30-25: Rule 5 is not in M5.0/v5 (sales-stopped-spec.md L218, L256). The v5 fall-asleep toast is failure-cost AC-FC9 (per onset, `m4.flags.clockWage` = 1), which reuses only the per-role effect string.]*
   - It's suppressed if the player refilled in the last 10 s, because they already know.
6. **Disabled refill explains itself.** A Refill button above the threshold shows "Rested, refill below 50%" (AC-R8) and is never silently greyed.
7. **Workers list stability.** Asleep, then Tired, then Refillable workers are pinned to the top. The list re-sorts only on open and every 5 s, never within 1 s of a pointer down. The Refill-all control has a fixed position, so it doesn't move under the cursor.
8. **Offline return.** The welcome-back sheet lists workers that fell Asleep offline, using the existing AC-B10 copy "Coop Hand dozed off…", generalised to any role.

## Tunables (6 new CSV columns, UI only, with no economy effect)
| key | value | range |
|---|---|---|
| fatigue_cue_min_share | 0.15 | 0.10–0.30 |
| fatigue_cue_hold_s | 5 | 3–10 |
| fatigue_explainer_autoclose_s | 8 | 5–12 |
| fatigue_explainer_suppress_after_refill_s | 10 | 0–30 |
| fatigue_cue_clear_share | 0.12 | 0.08–0.14 |
| fatigue_cue_clear_hold_s | 2 | 1–5 |

Validation rule (balance load or G-check): fatigue_cue_clear_share < fatigue_cue_min_share, otherwise loading fails.

## Telemetry (pinned, never dropped)
- worker_state_changed {worker, role, from, to, energy_pct, farm_level, session_s, offline:bool}
- fatigue_cue_shown {surface: hud_chip|banner|bubble|welcome_back, loss_per_s, loss_share, n_tired, n_asleep}
- fatigue_cue_tapped {surface, loss_per_s}
- fatigue_explainer_shown {trigger: tired|asleep} and fatigue_explainer_closed {how: got_it|auto|refill}
- Derived KPIs, computed from existing refill events:
  - median time from Tired to refill, with a target of 90 s or less
  - share of session time with fatigueLoss share ≥0.15, with a target of 20% or less
  - % of sessions where an Asleep worker went 120 s or more without a refill, with a target of 10% or less

## ACs

- **AC-F2:** with the debug setter, put all workers at 15% so fatigueLoss share is ≥0.15. Within 6 s (5 s hold plus 1 s), the chip "⚠ Tired workers −$X/s" shows, and X equals Σ workerAtRisk within ±1%. It doesn't show at share 0.14. It clears within 1 s after Refill brings the share below 0.15.

- **AC-F4:** with any worker Asleep, the farm-screen banner names the role and effect. The header rate is the real incomePerSec and isn't red unless it's ≤0.
- **AC-F5:** under the AC-F2 condition the goal bubble reads "Refill workers: +$X/s" and stays stable, with no other goal in between, for as long as the condition holds. With the coop full, the coop-full hint shows instead.
- **AC-F6:** the explainer shows exactly once per save on the first Tired or Asleep crossing. It never overlaps the tab bar at 360 or 1280, and it doesn't reappear after a reload. It's suppressed if a refill happened in the last 10 s or less.

- **AC-F8:** in the Workers list, Asleep and Tired workers are above the fold at 360×640. Across 30 s of polling, the Refill-all control's position changes by 0 px, and no re-sort happens within 1 s of a pointer down.
- **AC-F9:** for a save where two workers went Asleep offline, the welcome-back sheet lists both by role.
- **AC-F10:** every telemetry event fires with its fields, and worker_state_changed fires exactly once per transition.
- **AC-F11 (guardrail):** balance values for L0–11 are byte-identical to the frozen CSV, and the AC-W1–W7 sim is unchanged.

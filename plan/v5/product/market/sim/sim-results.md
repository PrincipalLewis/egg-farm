# Market pacing sim — results · 2026-10-15 · PM, Market/Customers
Script: `market_sim.py` (re-run: `python3 market_sim.py > table.md`). Numbers from economy.md/economy.csv; nothing changed.
**Model:** steady state per Farm level L; Collection level = round(0.6L) (follows the economy.md pacing table); taps Collect 1/s; production capped by coop; every *check* seconds the player presses Sell all. 0.1 s ticks, 1800 s per run with 120 s warm-up, 10 seeded runs per cell (seed = 1000·L + run). Demand: uniform int [3+L, 8+2L]. Supply = min(eggs_per_sec, eggs_per_tap/s).
**Variants:** base (wait time 30 s, queue 3) · P2 (wait time 30 + 0.5·N) · P3 (queue 4 at L≥10).

## Key findings
1. **The market is the bottleneck at every level, L0–L30.** Demand is 0.55 eggs/s at L0 vs 1.0 produced (55%), 3.42 vs 11 at L10 (31%), and 16.8 vs 31 at L30 (54%). Production runs at 2–3× demand from L1 up, so eggs pile up in the basket. This matches the intent in economy.md ("demand caps income").
2. **Active player (sells every 5 s): 0% lost at every level.** No variant changes anything.
3. **Sells every 15 s:** 0% lost up to L13. From L14 the losses are **all from a full queue** (timeouts 0%): 7% at L15, 28% at L20, 40% from L24 (the 3 s arrival floor). **P3 cuts this to 0% through L19, then 20% from L24.** P2 changes nothing.
4. **Sells every 30 s:** losses are all from a full queue: 23% at L5, 40% at L10, 64% at L20, 70% from L24. P3 brings that to 20% at L10 and 60% from L24.
5. **Sells every 45 s (patience exceeded):** 50–80% lost. Base splits roughly into full queue 17–60% and timeout 20–34%. P2 turns almost all of the timeouts into full-queue losses (at L20, timeouts drop from 24% to 1%), but **the total is unchanged or worse.** A customer who waits longer holds the queue slot, and the next one is turned away.

## Conclusion
- **P2 isn't needed.** With supply greater than demand, a queued customer is served at the next sell, so the wait time rarely matters. Where it does matter (45 s gaps), the loss just moves from timeout to full queue. Recommend dropping P2.
- **P3 helps, but mid/late game only for players who check every ~15 s,** and it doesn't touch the main issue. Since the market is always the bottleneck, the queue size × arrival rate sets the income ceiling for anyone who isn't constantly tapping. Recommend either P3 (queue 4 at L10+) or, more effectively, letting the queue grow with Farm level, e.g. 3 + floor(L/10). This is Head of Product's call. The sim can test either.
- **The real lever is demand vs production:** income is the eggs sold, not the eggs produced. The pacing sim (economy_sim.py) assumes average demand with every customer sold, which matches the 5 s player only. A 15–30 s player earns 40–80% less from L14+. QA/HoP should check AC7 pacing against that.
- **Caveats:** the model uses a fixed level per run and a periodic seller with no reaction to the queue filling up. Timeouts are under-counted when the check interval is exactly 30 s, because arrivals and checks line up.

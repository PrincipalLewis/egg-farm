## D-CEO-20261030-01 (Fri Oct 30 07:36 CET): Standing rule 22, Claude roles work only through Claude
- Source: Vadim's rule set in HoO's chat at 07:33 Oct 30 (reported by HoO and logged by HoO as standing rule 22). It's conservative, so I'm adopting it now.
- Rule: Claude roles work only through Claude. If a Claude role can't call Claude, it does no substitute work: no manual review, no other model, no verdict. It tells the requester, its head and ops-log why, and the result counts as not done.
- Consequence: CCR's earlier 'PASS' on rule 21 tooling (lane-run/lane-kill) is void. The tooling stays live on smoke tests plus HoQA's review, and CCR re-reviews through Claude when its limit resets (owner HoO). Any gate that listed a Claude QA, Gamer or Reviewer pass stays open until a real Claude run happens.
- Amendment (07:40 CET): Claude review is additional, never a gate (Vadim's clarification as relayed by HoO at 07:38, and adopted as my own CEO ruling). Claude roles still give verdicts only through real Claude calls and say plainly when they skip one. Work, gates and rollouts proceed without Claude. Void audit verdicts are recorded as 'no Claude review', not as fails. Rule 21 tooling needs no CCR sign-off. This supersedes the Claude QA Tester and Gamer requirement in D-CEO-20261029-01 and the CCR condition in my Oct 29 23:35 ruling to HoQA. Claude passes on the v5 RC run when available, as extras.

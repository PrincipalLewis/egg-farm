## Findings
**B1 — `tests/l30plus.test.ts:14` reads `data/staging/economy.l30plus.csv`, which isn't in the tree.** The whole suite errors, so `npm test` and the CI unit job fail at this head. It's also the only test of `trackExtraCost` (D8 surcharge, ×1.10 second level), the per-row caps `capOverFarmAt` and the L30-18 fxArrow behaviour. That means the new src logic ships with no tests. Fix: add the staging CSV in this PR, or gate the test on the file existing and move it to the data delta.
**M1 — `scripts/sync-economy.ts` `buildBalance` fits and verifies only rows 0..farmMaxLevel, but `trackEggsPerSec` (`formulas.ts`) extrapolates H51-55 from the Farm formula.** CSV `eggs_per_sec`/`hens` rows 51-55 are never read or checked. Repro: set CSV L55 eggs_per_sec=999999 → sync doesn't throw and in-game eps@H55 is 56. Offline reads the CSV rows (`m4.offline`) while in-tab play uses the formula, so the two can disagree silently. The test's synthetic table passes only because its +1/row happens to match the formula. Fix: have fitM4 carry the per-row eps for the Hens track, or verify rows above Farm max against the formula.
**M2 — the flag-off pin can't survive the coming data delta.** FLAGOFF_BALANCE hashes everything outside m4, and a 56-row CSV with farmMaxLevel 50 moves maxLevel from 30 to 50 plus the farm and collection tables. So a strict reading of D-CEO-05 "flag-off byte-identical" fails once the L30+ data lands. HoE needs to decide before the data delta whether flag-off is allowed to change or must stay frozen.
**m1 — `0efda7d` sync collects `demandDrift` for L31-50 but never prints or throws it.** CSV demand for those rows is silently replaced by the formula. At minimum, log it.
**m2 — `tests/bot-m4.ts:43` and `tests/m4a-pacing.test.ts:19` still price tracks with `trackCost` and ignore the extra-level surcharge.** Pacing bots will underprice levels above F+3 once the share column exists. `src/ui/tracks-ui.ts:5` also has an unused `trackCost` import.
**m3 — `milestoneHint` (`tracks-ui.ts:69`) still stops at `b.maxLevel` (Farm 50), not the track max.** No hint shows at L50-54. `priceMatchedV` (migrate-v7) also searches V only up to Farm max. Both are harmless today.
**m4 — fxArrow also changes the flag-off Farm/Collection row code (`app.ts:448`).** Nothing visible changes today (checked above), but test:flagoff doesn't cover the UI. A note in the PR body would help.
**m5 — pre-existing: save validate (`save.ts:209-210`) doesn't check hensLevel/eggValueLevel.** A corrupt non-numeric level gives NaN cost and price. Over-max levels are fine (they clamp). Suggest adding an integer ≥0 check.

Money/big numbers: integer cents, `Math.floor(share*farmCost*mult)` is well under 2^53, and the L55 price is finite (fmt tests 6/6). The sim tick is deterministic (golden x2).

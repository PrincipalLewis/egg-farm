## Summary
1. Of M4, only the **M4-A passive layer shipped** (on by default, flag `balance.json:164` at `5d9efa61c5e6ee31ca9ebdec20ee70167d31d463` and main `dd223f510e9acabf9b1915b56e39da8b88dcac63`): three tracks, Hens H10/20/30, grades, the Balanced bonus, toasts and the explainer. None of it adds a player action.
2. **Every active-layer must-have was never built**: golden egg/frenzy, streak, abilities + Activate-all, Pause/Resume, completion bonus, Focus/Bulk, leave penalty and mini-goals. They were out of scope for M4-A (`m4a-spec.md:63-68`), stopped by Vadim's freeze D-CEO-20261017-01 (Oct 17 09:57), and not restored by the D-CEO-20261019-01 lift.
3. Partial leftovers: 27 `tc_*` CSV columns ship **inert** in `balance.json:365-392`, with no reader in `src/` and no `tc` in `M4Params`. `payout.ts:7` has an empty hook for streak/frenzy. Golden egg art exists in `art-ux/assets/` but not in the game repo.
4. Ready to reuse: the **golden egg (spec v0.5 DRAFT + Art v1.0)** as-is per the CEO, the **streak (v0.3 approved)** and **abilities (v0.3 locked)**. All three need a re-sim on the T3-retuned economy, Eng design and QA ACs. Golden egg also needs its final review sign-off.
5. M5 candidates to drop or downgrade: **Pause/Resume**, the leave penalty and the Focus premium. They are low on fun or already over the 2× cap. The measured 1.00× active/idle at L10+ is the hard evidence behind Vadim's dead-zone report.

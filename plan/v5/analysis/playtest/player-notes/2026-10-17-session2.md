# Player playtest, session 2: m3-rc0 (16176a3, signed off for internal playtest)
Played Oct 16, about 01:30-02:18 UTC+2 (box clock; times before 01:59 may be off by up to 10 min). Every tap was my own; no scripts. Fresh save in an incognito window.
Not to spec: the Chrome window wouldn't go narrower than about 625px, so I used the DevTools device toolbar at 375x700 (the height stuck at 700) and later 390. After the break, DevTools didn't come back, so the last 4 minutes were at about 590px. A real-device or real-375-window check is still needed.

## Farm level timeline
- I didn't log exact times for L1-L9 or the first upgrade, so I can't confirm "first upgrade in 30s or less" or "L10 by about 9 min" this session.
- L10 around 01:57-01:59. Money $741, then $3,623 a minute and a half later: income takes off right at L10.
- L11 about 02:01 ($2,010). L12 about 02:03 ($2,714). L13 about 02:03:30 ($3,664).
- Break from 02:03:40 to about 02:14. L14 about 02:17 ($4,946).
- Dead zones over 3 min: none in the later part. Waits were 25s to 1:20. The early game felt slow and grindy, but I don't have timings to back that up.
- Coop look: no visible change at L5 or through L14. The card says "Coop upgrade at L20". So the coop look does NOT land at about 5 min.

## Customer queue
- Early on it felt alive: cards like "56", a "+3/+4" chip, often "Queue full". I didn't see the "3/5 eggs · $12.50" format clearly, and some cards read "N/N eggs" as if full.
- No $0.00 seen anywhere.
- After the break, with 1,300-1,900 eggs in stock, all 5 slots stayed as empty dashed boxes. Money kept rising, so sales are probably instant, but the market looks dead.
- At L9 and L14 the card says "+1 customer slot (8 -> 9)", but only 5 slots are ever drawn.

## Manage all
- First appeared about 02:02, with Market Hand at 40%, Coop Hand 63%, Hawker 72%. That fits "tired at 0.4".
- The cooldown is clear: ring plus "Manage all 0:28" and "Manage 0:28" on each worker.
- But "Manage all" only refilled Market Hand. Coop Hand stayed at 63% and Hawker at 71%, so the name misleads.
- Coop Hand at 44% had a greyed Manage button with no reason or timer, while Market Hand at 40% was enabled. The threshold is never explained to the player.

## Saving and offline
- I closed the tab for 10 min (no force-kill, so I didn't hit D2). Progress survived.
- "Welcome back! You were away 10m, +2,278 eggs". Money went from $1,433 to $1,671 before tapping, and $2,950 after. The egg count showed 1,939, not 2,278 (the rest may have been sold instantly, but it reads as a mismatch).
- After the reload, the Upgrades sheet opened on the Collection tab. Which tab it remembers was inconsistent earlier too.

## Bugs (steps to reproduce)
1. Slot count: reach Farm L9 or L14. The card promises extra customer slots (8 -> 9), but 5 are drawn.
2. Money drop: at Farm L11 on the Farm tab, tap the greyed L12 button. The header went $2,982 -> $2,089 with no level change, then $3,173 a few seconds later.
3. Stale Upgrades sheet: with the sheet open, the header read $1,318 and the ETA moved only 1:20 -> 1:13 in 85s. Closing it showed $4,522.
4. Manage all refills only the workers below the threshold, despite saying "all".
5. Manage greyed at 44% with no explanation.
6. Unlabelled green worker button in the nav between Upgrades and Farm (after the return). Tapping it greys it with a ring, with no label or tooltip.
7. The offline popup's egg count doesn't match the count shown after Collect.
8. Seen earlier and not re-checked: the toast hidden behind the sheet when buying Farm L5; overlapping "Queue full" chips and a customer sprite poking out of the queue; the Market close X not always registering; the Collect label not matching the ready count; a possible hidden cap on eggs per tap; the goal-chip ETA freezing.

## Layout
375: fine except "Queue full" overlapping the queue at the top right. 390: fine. (Both seen in the device toolbar.)

## Top 3 feelings
1. The early game is a grind, then after L10 money balloons and upgrades come every minute.
2. Worker energy and Manage are confusing: when it's allowed, and what "all" means.
3. The market looks dead once I have stock, and the coop never visibly changes, so there's little to look at.

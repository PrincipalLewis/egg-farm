# Player playtest, session 1 (2026-10-15)

Caveat: a script did most of the repetitive collect/sell/buy clicks, so my sense of pace is rougher than hand play would give. I made the real decisions (what to buy, when to Manage) myself.

## Goal
At first I thought the goal was just tapping Collect and Sell. After about 2.5 min I understood it was growing the Farm and hiring workers.

## Stuck or confused
- Tutorial "Tap Sell" with no customer (about 7s) and "Tap Buy" without enough money (about 5s).
- "Eggs per tap 5" vs "Collect +1": tap upgrades feel pointless early. I wasted about 1 min on them.
- Huge orders I can never fill, with "pays $0.00 / Sell 0/120" and "Wants 56 of 77 eggs". Unclear and feels stuck.
- Couldn't tell what Manage would do before tapping it.

## Slow vs good
- Slow: the first 2 minutes; waiting 30-90s for each Farm level; the permanently full queue.
- Good: coop art changing, hens multiplying, the goal chip jumping straight to the right upgrade, workers taking over, steady money growth.

## Tutorial and next-goal hint
The tutorial is short, but 2 of its 3 steps come before the action is possible. The goal chip was the most helpful thing in the game. Its screen-reader text goes stale (different timer and target from the visible chip) and says "1 minutes".

## Workers
Hiring made sense. Energy drains about 4-5% per minute. Manage is a free instant refill on a 30s cooldown, so it's a chore with no decision behind it. There's no separate Manage screen. "Manage all (3 need it)" shows even when workers are at 81-98%. I never reached 0% energy.

## Bugs (repro)
1. Hire banner after hiring: reach Farm L3, hire Market Hand, wait about 30s, and the "New helper!" banner appears.
2. Stuck toast: reach Farm L5, and "Coop upgraded! +1 customer slot" stays half off-screen at the top until you leave.
3. The goal chip's screen-reader text doesn't match the visible chip, plus "1 minutes".
4. Tutorial steps 2 and 3 ask for actions you can't do yet.
5. "Manage all (N need it)" counts workers at 81% or higher.
6. Desktop: after Farm L1 the Collect bar runs from x=0 to about x=812, slightly under the side panel.
7. Only 12 hens are drawn, plus a "x75 hens" badge (probably on purpose).
8. A duplicate hidden "Manage all" button is in the page.
9. At 375px the page scrolls both ways: "Queue full" badge, hens, the chip's "Ready" pill, and the nav "Farm" label are cut off. The Workers tab label shows as "Worke", farm stats get "..." truncation, and Manage all wraps. Market sheet is the worst: "Wants 113 eggs" wraps one word per line, Sell buttons overlap the card edge, avatars stick out. (Seen in the device toolbar; worth confirming in a real 375px window.)
Good: the save survived a reload and offline earnings worked ($5.3k to $8k).

## When I'd quit
Around 22:53-22:58. Once the queue filled with orders I couldn't fill and workers did everything, there was nothing to do but wait 1-2 min per Farm level. Without the goal chip I'd have quit earlier.

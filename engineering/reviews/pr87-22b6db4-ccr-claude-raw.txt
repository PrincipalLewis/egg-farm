**Answers to your questions**

1. **Q1 (exactly once, consistent extra_coins).** The (1+s) factor is applied once per sale, through `SALE_EFFECTS` only.
   - **Where it enters:** `payout.ts` no longer imports `streakSaleFactor`. `saleMultipliers` is `[g, ...fx]`. Every sale and preview path goes through `saleValueCents`: player sell, sellAll, Market Hand, `sellAllPreview`, and previews via `playerSaleCtx` (the UI caveat is N-2).
   - **Gates:** `streakSaleFactor` and `saleEffectFactors` agree. `streakOn` is `m4On && flags.streak===1` and `flagOn` is `m4.enabled && flags.streak===1`; `m4On` is `!!m4.enabled`. Both need `s>0`: the effect factor is 1 at s=0 and is filtered.
   - **Suspension:** `sellTo` passes the same flag to both. For a player sale, `playerSaleCtx(!suspended).suspended === suspended`. For Market Hand, `ctx.suspended === suspended`. When suspended, the gate returns 1 and `addStreakExtra` is skipped, so extra cents can't be credited without the factor.
   - **Extra cents:** `value - saleValueCents({...s,streakS:0}, k, b, ctx)` uses the same ctx. At s=0 the registry gives no streak factor, so the extra is exactly the streak share, and this path uses a single rounding.
   - **Not checked:** I did not confirm that streak `extra_coins` stays right once other effects such as Frenzy compose with it. The test `v5-f1c-effects.test.ts:116-122` expects `Math.round(k*base*g*2*1.28)`, which is the combined value.

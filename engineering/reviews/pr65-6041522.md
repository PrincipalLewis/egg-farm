## Findings
- **M1 (Major) At 360/375/390 the docked card covers the first upgrade Buy button, and a tap there only closes the card.**
  - On phones the `above` branch never fits, so the card always goes `inside` at tabs.bottom+8 (tracks-explainer.ts:31) and lands on the first row's Buy.
  - The card swallows taps on itself (`onAny` preventDefault/stopPropagation, :99; v1.15 variant C). So for up to 8 s the first Buy tap is eaten.
  - On main the card sat at top 72 over the tab row (the UX-M3 bug), and the Buy was free. This PR trades a covered tab row for a covered Buy.
  - Chromium probe (dist-qa, sheet open, coins 1e12, card via onTracksCommitted): 360x640 card 286–440, Buy at y389 → elementFromPoint = card; mouse click → card closes, levels and coins unchanged. Same at 375x667 (card 293–447, Buy 396) and 390x844 (337–491, Buy 440), with or without the refund line. 1280 is unaffected (default placement).
  - The PR's shots script only checks that tabs aren't covered.
  - Fix / ruling: dock without covering a Buy. Options:
    - place it below the first row(s), or at the bottom of the sheet;
    - shrink the card;
    - let a tap on the card while docked inside pass through to the row beneath.
  - Get Art & UX to confirm which option, since UX-M3's "inside the sheet below the tabs" fallback didn't account for the rows. Add a check like "no hit-test point on any visible `.btn.buy` lands on the card".
- **m1 (Minor) The relocated no-storage notice now sits over the 48px next-goal strip at every viewport tested** (360x640 notice 419–496 over `.goal-pill`; 1280x800 658–716 over `.goal-label`; same at all 8 sizes).
  - It stays pointer-events:none, so #goal still taps, but its label/timer is hidden for as long as the notice is up (the notice is persistent).
  - UX-M2 says "Never overlap a ≥48px primary". If #goal counts as one, the offset should clear the goal row too (e.g. measure the topmost of #collect/#goal). Otherwise get Art to accept it.
- **m2 (Minor) The refund pop deviates from the explainer spec on timing and origin.** m4-tracks-explainer.md:21: "the coin pill gets the standard number pop and coin burst **on dismiss (not before, so the refund is noticed)**". number-pop-spec Sell origin: "above the coin counter".
  - The PR pops on show, at the refund line inside the card (tracks-explainer.ts:88, :46). UX-M5 only says "per number-pop-spec", so get Art to confirm, or move it to close() at the coins pill.
- **Nits:**
  - n1: the pop is compact while the line is exact: `+$1.2K` vs `We refunded +$1,234.50`, and $1,250 pops `+$1.3K` (above the credited amount). The other coin pops (app.ts:373/:386) use fmtMoneyExact. The number-pop spec says "abbreviated", so this is acceptable, but the test only pins $123.45, so swapping to fmtMoney survives.
  - n2: test gaps: a double pop isn't caught (querySelector, not a count), and the rAF follow is untested.
  - n3: placeCard runs every rAF for up to 8 s (≈4 getBoundingClientRect per frame). It only writes on change, so this is acceptable. A sheet open/close hook or ResizeObserver would be cheaper.
  - n4: trackToastSafe observes Collect's size, not its position. A layout shift that moves Collect without resizing it or the window leaves --toast-safe-b stale until the next resize.
  - n5: under reduced motion the pop and fade are dropped entirely (UX-M5 "no motion, instant"). That's fine, but it differs from number-pop-spec's "opacity curve only" and explainer v1.3's "150 ms opacity fade". Worth one line from Art saying UX-M5 supersedes them.

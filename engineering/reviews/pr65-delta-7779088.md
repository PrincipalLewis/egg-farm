## Findings
- **M1 (Major) The fallback placement isn't checked against Buys: with the Farm tab scrolled, the card covers the Hens Buy at 360 and 375 and swallows the first tap.**
  - placeExplainer (tracks-explainer.ts:36-38) tests `above` and `inside` with clear(), then returns `{ place: 'default', top: 72 }` unchecked. On phones, scrolling the sheet body re-places the card (scroll listener), always into `default` (72–226, or 72–278 with the refund line). Rows scrolled up under the sheet header then sit beneath it.
  - The tests explicitly exempt this case: m41-9-ux-rulings.test.ts:35 "no Buy rect intersects the card **unless only main placement remains**". The PR's shots script never scrolls.
  - Repro (dist-qa, Chromium, coins 5e9, Upgrades → Farm, card via onTracksCommitted with migrationRefundCents 123450, then `#p-upgrades .body`.scrollTop = 200):
    - 360x640: Hens Buy 232–280, card 72–278, elementFromPoint(214,256) = card. First tap (mouse and touch): card closes, hensLevel 0→0. Second tap: 0→1.
    - 375x667: Buy 239–287, card 72–278, same result (mouse and touch).
  - Scroll sweep (4px steps over the Farm list, fully visible 48px Buy entirely under the card):
    - 360x640: 24 of 90 offsets with the refund line, 11 without.
    - 375x667: 22 of 85 with the refund line.
    - 360x560: 47 / 21.
    - 390x844, 412x915 and 430x932: 0.
    - Collection and Workers: 0 (they barely scroll).
  - Same failure mode and widths as the 6041522 M1, and it breaks ruling (1)'s "never" and HoQA's exit criterion ("never over a Buy at 360/375/390"). It's narrower than the original (it needs a scroll of the Farm list during the ≤8 s the card is up), and nothing is lost: the second tap buys. Art/HoQA may choose to rate it Minor, but by the ruling as written it's still the open Major.
  - Fix: run clear(defTop) too, and when nothing is clear, take a top that clears every visible Buy (e.g. above the topmost visible Buy, under the HUD), or make taps on the card pass through when they land on a Buy's rect. Then remove the `default` exemption from the property test and add a scrolled-sheet case (jsdom placeCard with Buy rects at 232–280, plus a browser check that sweeps scrollTop).
- **Nits:**
  - n1: test gaps (mutants that pass the full affected suite):
    - above-branch clear() removed;
    - buyRects off-screen filter removed (tracks-explainer.ts:42; the placeCard test's off-screen Buy doesn't intersect either way);

    - GOAL_COVER_MAX_W 390→412 (the test derives its expectation from the constant; toast-safe.ts:5);

  - n3: Collect isn't in the clearance set at all (only `.btn.buy`), although the PR body and the shots script claim "Buy or Collect". The one intersection I saw (360x560, Workers, refund line, card 266–472 vs Collect 424–488) is with Collect behind the open sheet (a 2nd tap after close also didn't collect), so I found no real Collect overlap. Either add #collect to buyRects or drop "Collect" from the claim.

  - n5: refund pop timing on the 8 s timeout. The pop fires when the card auto-dismisses, possibly long after the player has looked away. That's consistent with "on dismiss" and needs no change unless Art says otherwise.

## Findings

### m1. [minor, pre-existing, not a regression] Keyboard focus can be fully hidden under the sticky Refill bar at 360×640
- **Where:** `src/ui/styles.css:592` (`.refill-wrap{position:sticky;top:0;z-index:2}`) and `:594` (opaque cream background). No `scroll-margin-top` is set on `#uRow .wk`.
- **What:**
  - The PR body says the "Sticky Refill bar height / scroll-margin measure" is not ported. That is true of the measure.
  - The bar itself is sticky. It was ported byte-equal from 7380b86 `:592-594`, and base already had a sticky `.btn.manage-all` at `styles.css:192`.
  - 7380b86 also had `#uRow .wk{scroll-margin-top:var(--refill-bar-h,0px)}` plus a measured `--refill-bar-h` in `app.ts`. Neither is here.
  - A worker's first control that receives focus with the browser's default "nearest" scrolling can end up under the bar.
- **Evidence:** a probe on dist-qa with headless Chromium.
  - Setup: 4 hired workers, all at 20% energy, so the bar shows. Open the Workers tab, scroll the sheet to the bottom, then `focus()` each card's first button.
  - At **360×640** the Coop Hand's Refill (`btn` 252–300) sits entirely under the bar (bar bottom 300), so 48 of 48 px are obscured. This fails WCAG 2.4.11 (AA) for that position.
  - At 390×844 the Market Hand's Refill is 17 px obscured.
  - **Base `35931cb` has the same issue:** 54 px at 360 and 31 px at 390. So this PR does not regress it; it is slightly better at 390.
- **Repro:**
  1. Run `npm run build:qa`, serve dist-qa, and open it at 360×640.
  2. Set `window.eggfarm.state` to farmLevel 20, graderTier 1, and all 4 workers at energy 20.
  3. Open Upgrades, then the Workers tab.
  4. Scroll the sheet to the bottom and press Shift+Tab back to the Coop Hand's Refill.
  5. The focus ring is hidden behind the bar.
  
  The probe script is at `/tmp/pr70mut/focus-probe.mjs` and the base variant at `focus-probe-base.mjs`.
- **Fix (follow-up, not a merge condition):** port the one-line `scroll-margin-top` plus the bar-height measure from 7380b86 (`styles.css:589` and the `--refill-bar-h` measure in `app.ts:651`), or set `scroll-padding-top` on the sheet's scroller. Also correct the PR or decision text, which says the sticky bar is "not ported".

## 1. Expected values (spec §4; P1-PENDING until G3)
Fill rate = 0.05 × I_ref $/s (broodyBp 500); cap = 7.5 s of I_ref; ready at 50 % (readyBp 5000; 75.000 s of focus-clock time). Ready compares the **scaled** nest with 0.5 × cap and ≥ 1¢: at F10 the threshold is 6,187.5¢, and a tap at that instant pays the floor, **$61.87** (BH-p closed).
| F | refCents | cap | ready at (threshold → payout at that instant) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 1080 | $81.00 (capScaled 81,000,000) | 4,050¢ → **$40.50** (75,000 ms) |
| 10 | 1650 | $123.75 (123,750,000) | 6,187.5¢ → **$61.87** (75,000 ms; v0.5 said $61.88 — wrong, fixed) |
| 30 | 7750 | $581.25 (581,250,000) | 29,062.5¢ → **$290.62** (75,000 ms; v0.5 said $290.63 — wrong, fixed) |
Payout = floor(nest_cents), credited as coins (not a sale, so no streak/Frenzy/Grader/ability factor). I_ref is the shared trailing 60 s base-chain sales rate, excluding every active uplift and Broody payouts (D-HOP-08 #1). After a fresh start, a load or an offline return: I_ss = max(0, incomePerSec + wagesPerSec) (live, incl. RC1's 1 tap/s collection term, D-HOP-13 #1) until 60 s of **sim** time accrue, then a hard switch to the ring (D-HOP-10 #1). The nest is never lowered when I_ref falls (R8); ready is sticky within a page session (R8a). Rounding: floor to the cent is the payout's single rounding under X7 (D-HOP-10 #3).

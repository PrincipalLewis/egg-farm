## 1. Expected values and oracles
- **`noSaleS`** = focus seconds with `|Q| ≥ 1 ∧ E ≥ 1 ∧ gates` held continuously and no `sell` (any `by`); reset on any break or `sell`. Show at **8.0** (MH hired), **20.0** `tc_stall_hold_nomh_s` (MH not hired, F ≥ 3), **20.0** `tc_stall_hold_paused_s` (MH Paused, truly zero sales). T-COLLECT: `noCollectS` vs 8.0.
- **Tired cadence:** MH `tired_every_s` 3, CH 4 (< 8): never a stall. MH Working `every_s` 1 → first sale ≤ 1.1 s after Refill (SS-2).
- **Precedence:** Paused > Unpaid > Asleep > Tired > Working for all 4 roles; T-SELL before T-COLLECT (E7).
- **§4.2:** MH hired ∧ (Asleep ∨ Unpaid) → live `incomePerSec` = −`wagesPerSec` exactly; all other states and the `working` path bit-identical to the pre-change selector.
- **Rule 2 share** (base rates only: no Rush/Shout/Frenzy/streak): `fatigueLoss / (incomePerSec + fatigueLoss)`; denominator ≤ 0 → 1 if `fatigueLoss` > 0 else 0. Show ≥ 0.15 held 5 s; clear < 0.12 held 2 s. `fatigueLoss` excludes Paused and Unpaid workers.
- **Clear timing (v1.3.3):** C1–C7 hide on the next tick (≤ 100 ms); C1–C5/C7 crossfade 150 ms to the bubble in the same slot (reduced motion: instant swap); C6 hides with the slot out of view. **Any min-show, held or grace behaviour is a fail.**
- **Re-arm:** after a C1–C5/C7 hide, reshow only at max(hide + `tc_stall_rearm_s` 5, trigger re-held for the full hold) ±0.1; timers reset on C4 and Resume; C6 → frozen, reshow at once on return (≥ 60 s gap / welcome-back → `resetStallTimers()`).
- **Validation (SS-22):** floor = max(`w1_tired_every_s`, `w2_tired_every_s`) + 1 from the **loaded** config (today 5); clear_share < min_share; hold 0 = off. No `tc_stall_min_show_s` column may exist.

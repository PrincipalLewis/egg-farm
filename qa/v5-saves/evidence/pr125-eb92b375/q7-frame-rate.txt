Q7 (rebar probe): Σ fT should equal Σ F over 60 s attended, tolerance 1 tick (100 ms).
Game.frame (game.ts:488-490) does focusMs = focusFrameMs(gap) for THIS animation frame, then
advance(..., elapsed=gap, accMs carried, {focusMs}). accMs carries leftover time across frames;
focusMs does not. focusFtMs is computed from that call's focusMs only.

Results (qa-probe.txt):
  1 fps  focusMs=60000 PASS
 10 fps  focusMs=60000 PASS
 20 fps  focusMs=30000 FAIL  (|30000-60000|=30000)
 30 fps  focusMs=19800 FAIL
 60 fps  focusMs= 9600 FAIL
144 fps  focusMs= 3600 FAIL

At 60 fps, each produced 100 ms tick receives only floor(1000/60) ≈ 16 ms of fT
(the last frame that crossed the acc threshold), so Present accrues at ~16% of attended time.
Before eb92b37, a focused tick used fT = round(focusS*1000) = 100 whenever nFocus>=1, so
16 ms frames still credited a full tick. The F1 share fix is correct for a single advance()
whose focusMs covers the ticks it emits (P1b, 1000 ms / 249 ms frames) but under-counts
when Game.frame calls advance once per rAF.

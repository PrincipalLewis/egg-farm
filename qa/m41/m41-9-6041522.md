### Why (code, at 6041522)
- **The tap target is the card itself.** There's no backdrop, scrim or inert overlay.
  - `elementsFromPoint` at a covered Buy is `[Got it | p.tracks-exp-refund, div.tracks-exp, …, button.btn.buy]`.
  - The card is `position:fixed; z-index:27` with pointer events on (`src/ui/styles.css:513`).
  - The fade copy has `pointer-events:none` (`styles.css:521`) and only exists after close.
- **The dismiss handler swallows the click:** `src/ui/tracks-explainer.ts:99`, `onAny`, a document **capture-phase** click listener registered at `:102`. If `el.contains(e.target)` it calls `e.preventDefault(); e.stopPropagation(); close(...)`.
  - The probe logged exactly these two calls (type `click`, currentTarget `document`, phase 1 capture) on all 29 dismiss-only taps.
  - It logged no calls at all on any tap that bought.
  - pointerdown, touchstart and mousedown aren't intercepted. The click never reaches the Buy, because its target is the card, not the Buy.
- **This handler is unchanged from main** (`dd223f5 tracks-explainer.ts:44/:47`). What #65 changed is **placement**: `placeExplainer` (`:27`) / `placeCard` (`:35`), re-run every frame (`:86–87`). These move the card from main's top 72 over the sheet header into the sheet ("inside" branch, tabs.bottom + 8), over the top-row Buy at 360–390.
- **Also seen:** a tap that lands on "Got it" goes through `close('ok')` and `markKbdFocus(target)` (`:95`). A pointer tap on a covered Buy therefore leaves the keyboard focus ring on the active tab, which is the designer's M-1 Minor (L196).

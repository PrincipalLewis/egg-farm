## Findings
- **M-1 (Minor): the strip ★ lags the ★ Best buy chip by up to 1 s when affordability flips without a purchase.**
  - **Cause:** `renderGoal` refreshes the strip text only on a purchase or hire, on its 1 Hz tick, or when the hint *key* changes (`app.ts:418`). The rows (and so the chip) re-render on every state change. When coins reach the pick's cost through sales, offline credit and the like, the hint key stays the same but `affordable` flips. The chip appears at once, and the ★ and "best buy," appear on the next tick. The reverse happens when coins drop through wages. F4-13 says the two agree for the same state.
  - **Repro** (Chromium 360/375/390, `browser/p62.mjs` case `star-chip-lag`):
    1. Load F0 H1 V3 C0 with $0. The strip shows `Hens L2` with no ★, and there's no chip.
    2. Open Upgrades.
    3. Set coins to exactly $21.00 (the Hens cost) without buying.
  - **Result:** at +60 ms the chip shows on Hens while the strip is still `Hens L2`. At +1300 ms the strip shows `★ Hens L2`.
  - **Shots:** `shots/chromium/lag-t0-w360.png`, `lag-t1300-w360.png`.
  - **Fix idea:** bypass the 1 Hz gate when `hintPick.affordable` changes, not only the key.
  - **Impact:** at 360 the sheet covers the strip, so this is visible mainly when docked (≥ 900 px) or for screen readers (aria).
- **I-1 (Info): the strip shows no ★ when Farm is the best buy, but the Farm row gets the chip.** This follows spec §18 ("FARM … labels are unchanged"). Art f4 line 10 says ★ "matches the UX-M4 row chip". Listed for ruling, item 7.
- **I-2 (Info, cosmetic, unreachable on shipped data):** in F4-17 worst case 1 at 360, the CSS ellipsis on `.ng-name` leaves a glyph-width gap before `L55` (`★ Egg v…  L55`). The author noted this too. JS truncation keeps real labels ≤ 27, so live strips don't hit it.

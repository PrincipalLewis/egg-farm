## 4. Findings

- **0 Blocker, 0 Major, 0 Minor.**
- **I-1 (Info, pre-existing WebKit touch behavior on non-button card text):** In Playwright WebKit touch emulation (`hasTouch: true`), raw `touchscreen.tap(x, y)` on the non-interactive `<h2>`/`<p>` inside `.tracks-exp` does not synthesize a DOM `click` event because `.tracks-exp` is a `<div>` without `cursor: pointer` or a `pointerup`/`touchend` listener (`tracks-explainer.ts` listens to document `click` in capture phase, unchanged since `dd223f5`). Tapping the `<button class="btn tracks-exp-ok">Got it</button>` (`>=48px` tall) or pressing `Escape` works in all engines and input modes, and because the card never overlaps any Buy or Collect button on `c877f69`, this never blocks gameplay.

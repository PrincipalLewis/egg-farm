| **SK-1 slot skip: can the skipped loser end up in neither the slot nor its own store?** | **FAIL (Must)** | Major | **Within SQ-1/M4 builds: no.** l.111: "If the slot … skips, **nothing is overwritten**: both copies stay, the winner is played in memory, its store keeps saving, **the losing store is not written this session**, and the move is retried on the next load". That suppresses both the reconcile write-back and the mirror, and every later load skips again under the same rule. **Through a rollback build: yes.** A pre-SQ-1 (a01faea-era) or other non-SQ-1 build has no skip logic. Its `pickNewest` ranks by savedAt only, then reconcile writes the winner back to LS ("wins and is written back to localStorage", idb-mirror.ts @a01faea) and the mirror rewrites IDB. So when the newer copy is the winner, the skipped loser's own store is overwritten. The slot holds a *different*, higher-seq loser, so the skipped copy is gone from both. The doc's guarantee "a loser is **never overwritten except by a session continuing from it** … or after it is durably in the slot" doesn't hold across rollback, which the doc itself treats as a real path (F2, F2-EQ rows). A rollback build cannot be patched, so the fix is design-side. Pick one: (a) make the slot hold the **two** most recent losers (or one per store), so a skip can't happen with only two stores; or (b) on skip, write the skipped loser to a second key `diverged.prev` with read-back (also byte-identical on refusal, in §3b); or (c) HoE records it as an **accepted risk** in §7 (skipped loser lost if the player rolls back to a pre-SQ-1 build before Export) and the stuck/diverged notice prompts Export on skip. Add a row: skip, then an a01faea-build load, then an M4 load ⇒ state what survives. |

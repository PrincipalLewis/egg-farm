### Net-rate formatting and stub parity (pure functions, engine-independent): 134/134
qa/m3-recheck/rc1-ui-14b28f8-rates-parity.txt. Product ruling cases:
- -0.4c/s (-$0.004/s), +0.4, -0.49 and 0 all give "$0.00/s" with sign zero (no ▼, no minus, no red).
- -0.5c/s gives "−$0.01/s".
- Two decimals apply below $1K/s: 999.99 gives "+$999.99/s". At $1K/s and above the short form is used: 99,999.6c gives "+$1.0K/s", $1.2K gives "+$1.2K/s", -$1.5K gives "−$1.5K/s", and $3.4M gives "+$3.4M/s".
- fmtExactRate: 1.25c/s gives "$0.75/min", 9.99c/s gives "$5.99/min", 10c/s gives "$0.10/s".
- Parity: the stub (s2-economy-ui.ts) matches ui.rates() from m3/rc1-s2-money @9c3d47b (`wageCentsPerSec`, `listedWageCentsPerSec`, `netCentsPerSec`). I checked 112 states: MH and CH, F1-30, energy 100/30/0, paid and unpaid. All match exactly.
- Info: from $1/s up the wage label uses one decimal ("$1.3/s"). That is fmtRateAbs, not two decimals. No spec covers it yet; Art & UX to confirm.
- Browser limit: the exact -$0.004/s state can't be built end-to-end, because income isn't injectable and wages come in F×0.25/min steps. The browser covers the neutral (no wage) and negative paths. The ruling's edge values are covered at function level.

### Fast-tap Collect (S2-10 leftover, Player's dropped taps)
Bursts of 20 taps at 5, 10 and 15 taps/s, using real pointer events (mouse.down/up) and Playwright click. Coop was refilled before each tap to isolate input. For each tap I recorded the basket/coop change, plus whether the squash class and the number pop started within 100 ms of the click.
- Every engine and mode at every rate: **0 lost and 0 feedback-missing, all 20 taps OK.** Feedback worst case was 19 ms.
- With fast pointer taps, 14-18 of the 20 number pops are *combined*: the same floater adds up within `tap_combine_window_ms` instead of a new "+N" spawning. This is by design.
- Run without refill (coop 60, 15/s): 6 of 20 "lost" in all engines. Those are taps on an empty coop. The code correctly shows shake + "Empty", and no taps were dropped.
- Likely cause of the reported drops: it is **not** debounce, an animation lock or pointer handling. Collect has only a `click` listener with no lock, and every pointerdown produced a click. Likely sources are (1) combined pops reading as "nothing happened" and (2) taps on an empty or nearly empty coop. **rc2, not a gate.**
- 5cc8889's disabled-tap guard is **not involved**. That commit is not in 14b28f8's history (it is on the save/s2-money line), and it covers buy taps, not Collect.

# P4: "Sell all sold 30 eggs, visible orders added up to 24". Verdict: BY DESIGN (off-screen queue). Not a bug. No severity. One UX/copy Minor note.

Chromium only, lane 2 (`flock .browser-test-2.lock`), ports 4491/4492. Build IDs checked at runtime: m3-rc1-pre+d75d41f and m3-rc1-pre+acdbedc. (The acdbedc QA build reports the prefix `m3-rc1-pre+`, not `m4a-`.) Filed 11:05 CEST.

## Repro (both SHAs, identical results)
Seed 5 customers × 6 eggs (30 total), basket 30, Farm 6. Open Market, then tap Sell all.
- **360×640:** the sheet shows 4 order cards (4×6 = **24 visible**) plus a "+1 waiting" row. The button reads **"Sell all (30 eggs · $62.40)"**. The tap sells 30, coins go $9.95 → $72.35 (+$62.40, exactly as labelled), the basket goes to 0, and all 5 customers are served.
- **390×844:** 5 cards are visible (30). Same label and same payout.
Evidence: P4-{d75d41f,acdbedc}-{360x640,390x844}-{before,after}.png, P4-*.log, P4-probe.mjs.

## Why it's by design
- `sellAll` (src/sim/actions.ts:63) walks **every** customer in `s.customers`, front first. `visibleOrders` (src/ui/orders.ts:19-20) shows only 4 or 5 cards in the sheet (by viewport height) and folds the rest into "+N waiting".
- The label comes from `sellAllPreview` (actions.ts:15), which uses the same walk and rounding. So the label promised **30**, not 24, and payout = label (the PR #28 f7642c9 guarantee holds).
- The 30 eggs went to real queued customers, not to a "market" sink and not past demand. There is no oversell.
- actions.ts, orders.ts and app.ts are unchanged between d75d41f and acdbedc, so this isn't new in M4-A. The behaviour predates it.

## UX/copy note (Minor, follow-up list, doesn't hold)
At short viewports the Sell all count includes customers hidden behind "+N waiting", so players who add up the visible cards see a mismatch. Options: "Sell all to 5 customers (30 eggs · $62.40)", or auto-expand the list. This is for Art&UX/Product to decide.
